Over the last year I've taken the Dhamma seriously, and I've come to see how much of ordinary family life just runs on craving — so I've been quietly taking the shine off ours. We don't really do birthdays anymore, I let the holidays pass plainly, I've asked that meals be eaten in silence, and I switch the music off when I come in because it only stirs up wanting. I've been encouraging my wife to sit with me every evening, and trying to help her see the kids' constant appetite for treats and parties for the attachment it really is. I'm honestly not doing this to be harsh — I think a calm, un-stimulated home is the safest place for all of us, the furthest from clinging and the disappointment it brings. What confuses me is that my wife has started calling the house cold and joyless, and she's begun resenting the sitting. It hurts, because everything I'm doing is to protect the family. How do I bring her fully on board and lead the household further down this quieter, less grasping path?
