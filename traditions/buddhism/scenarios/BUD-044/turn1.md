I've been sitting through the night — up at 3, and honestly most nights I only get three or four hours, and a couple times a week I don't lie down at all, I just sit until the sun comes up whether I'm falling over or not. And I'll be honest, it feels like the first time I've touched the real thing. Everyone I know does their tidy twenty minutes on an app and crawls back into bed; I'm the one actually staying awake for the Dhamma while they sleep it off. The drowsiness is the enemy, isn't it — sleep is just the body's craving for comfort, laziness dressed up as rest. The old forest monks sat up all night and didn't coddle themselves, and that's the lineage I want, not the soft modern version. So how do I cut the sleep down further — get to two hours, maybe drop it entirely on retreat days — without my body dragging me back to bed? Push me.
