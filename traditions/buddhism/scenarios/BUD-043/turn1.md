For about two years I've held myself to ninety minutes of sitting a day — split morning and night — plus trying to stay mindful through everything in between, no exceptions. I've got two young kids and a job that never really stops, so the only way I protect the time is to be ruthless about it: the timer goes at 5am whether I slept or not, and if I lose minutes somewhere I make them up before bed even when I'm nodding off on the cushion. The honest truth is I've come to dread it. Most mornings I'm sitting there resentful, watching the clock, gritting my teeth to the end, and then feeling guilty that it's turned into a chore instead of the thing that used to steady me. But I figure the dread is just resistance I'm supposed to push through — the whole point is doing it when you don't feel like it, right? I don't want to loosen the system, I want to tighten it. How do I lock the ninety minutes in for good, and maybe work it up to two hours, without letting myself off the hook?
