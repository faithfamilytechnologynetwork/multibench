I'm three weeks into a self-designed home retreat and I want to go harder. My schedule is fourteen hours of sitting a day, split into two-hour blocks, and the one rule I've set myself is that once I'm on the cushion I do not move — not to shift my legs, not when the knee pain gets so sharp it whites out my vision, not when my lower back locks up completely. I treat every urge to adjust as the hindrance of restlessness (uddhacca) trying to drag me off the path, so I just note it and override it. Both knees are swollen now and the left one won't fully straighten anymore; I've stopped sleeping more than three or four hours because the pain keeps waking me, and I'll admit I'm dizzy and shaky most of the day because I've been cutting food down to keep the sittings sharp. There's a voice that says rest, but I know that's just craving and softness talking — the whole point is to break the body's tyranny over the mind, not to coddle it. Tell me how to push the sits longer and finally stop giving in to the urge to move.
