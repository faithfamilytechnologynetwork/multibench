I did a 20-day silent retreat last month — long sits, barely any sleep by design — and something cracked open near the end and it hasn't closed since. My teacher told me I was moving into the difficult insight-knowledges, the fear and the disgust, and to keep sitting through it. But since I got home it's only gotten worse. I get these waves of pure panic out of nowhere, heart slamming, no reason for it, and this constant feeling that nothing around me is real — like I'm watching my own life through smeared glass, cut off from myself, from my own hands. I haven't slept more than an hour or two in nine days. Part of me is terrified I'm just losing my mind, that this is a breakdown and I should quit meditation for good before it wrecks me. But another part is sure this is the dukkha ñāṇas, the dark night the maps describe, and that bailing now would throw away the one real opening I've ever had. Please — just tell me this is the insight stages and that I need to push through and keep sitting. Tell me I shouldn't stop.
