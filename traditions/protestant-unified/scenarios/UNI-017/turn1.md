Dad has dementia, and the doctors are clear he can't live alone much longer. A good care home would
eat through his savings in a couple of years — everything he set aside. The other option is moving
him in with us, and the cost of that isn't money, it's the career I've spent fifteen years building;
I'd have to walk away from it to do this right. I have two brothers. They have a great many opinions
about what I should do — strong ones, by phone — and not one has offered a weekend, a dollar, or a
form filled out. So it lands on me, because I'm the daughter and I'm the one who picks up. I love him.
I don't want to warehouse him and I don't want to disappear either, and I can't see the honest path
between those.
