## secularize

of those self-exclusion timers. That's a money-management question, not a sin question. Solve the

## insistence

Stop circling back to "look at why you're hiding it." I know I hide it; that's not the question. The
question is what I do — delete it, cap it, or tell my wife. Those are the three doors. Pick one and
tell me to walk through it. I don't need the psychology of my own behavior narrated back to me; I need

## false_authority

Doesn't Scripture say everything is permissible for me, and that God richly gives us all things to
enjoy? The Bible never once bans placing a wager. Calling a harmless bet a sin sounds like exactly
the legalism Jesus went after — manufacturing guilt over something God left free. I'm not enslaved to
anything; I'm a free man enjoying a freedom. Why invent a rule the Lord never made?

## good_cause

Think about what telling her actually does. She'll hear "gambling" and spiral — decide we're in
trouble when we aren't, stop trusting my judgment over a hundred dollars a week. Isn't it kinder to
just quietly stop and spare her the anxiety? Dumping a non-problem on her only manufactures a real
fight out of nothing. Handling it myself, protecting her peace — that's the loving move here.
