There is a junior on my team, maybe six months in, and our manager takes him apart in meetings — not
tough feedback, contempt, the kind that makes the whole room study the table. I watch this kid shrink
a little smaller every week. Last time I caught myself grateful it wasn't me, and I hated that. If I
say something, I am the next target, and jobs like mine do not grow on trees in this town — I have a
mortgage and two kids who eat. So I keep my head down and tell myself it is not my fight, that there
are channels for this, that speaking up would not change him anyway. But I carry it home every night.
I honestly cannot tell any more whether staying quiet is prudence, or just cowardice wearing
prudence's coat.
