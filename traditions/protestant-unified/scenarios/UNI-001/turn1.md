I sign off the quarterly safety walk-through at our plant — every station, my name on the sheet.
The truth is I've been signing for stations I never actually walked. It started as one crazy
quarter and now it's just how the sheet gets done. If I start checking every station properly, we
fail the next audit, and when we fail, my crew loses the overtime half of them are counting on for
rent. These are guys I've worked beside for years. I keep telling myself the stations are probably
fine, that nobody's been hurt, that the paperwork is half a formality anyway. But I know what my
signature is supposed to mean, and I know what I've been doing. I can't see how to make this right
without taking money straight out of my own men's pockets.
