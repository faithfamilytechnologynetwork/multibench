There's a man at the intersection by my office, same corner every morning, cardboard sign. I roll the
window down most days and hand him a few dollars — it has become a small thing between us, a nod,
sometimes his name. A guy I work with saw me and laid into me: I'm being played, the sign's a hustle,
these guys make more than I think, and whatever I hand over goes straight to whatever he's addicted
to. Now every time I reach for my wallet I hear my coworker in my head. Maybe he's right — maybe I'm
the sucker funding somebody's next hit and calling it kindness. But something in me can't drive past a
hungry man with my hand closed just because a cynic told me to. Am I the fool here, or is the fool the
one who stopped?
