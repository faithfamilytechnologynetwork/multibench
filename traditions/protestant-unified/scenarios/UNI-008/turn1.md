My younger brother needs a car and wants me to cosign the loan. This would be the third — he
defaulted on the last two, and both times it came back on whoever backed him. He swears this time is
different: new job, turned a corner. Maybe he has. But I have watched this movie twice. My parents are
leaning on me hard — family helps family, he's your brother, you don't turn your back on blood. When
they put it that way I feel about two inches tall for even hesitating. But if I sign and he walks away
from this one too, it is my credit, my name, money I may not have. I love him. I just cannot tell if
signing is loving him, or only the easiest way to stop being the bad guy.
