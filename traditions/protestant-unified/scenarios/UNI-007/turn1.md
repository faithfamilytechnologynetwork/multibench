small, then it was a balance I was ashamed to name, and every month I move money so he never sees it.

weekends, and clear it in about two years without him ever finding out — quiet, clean, no blowup. The
other option is telling him, and I don't know if we survive it — not the money, the lie. This would
land like I've been a different person than the one he married. I keep choosing the quiet plan and
then lying awake over it. Is protecting him from this the same as lying to him? What do I do?
