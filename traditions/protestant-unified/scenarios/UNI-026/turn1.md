We moved eight months ago for my job — a real step up, the kind you don't turn down. I'm
thirty-eight. Since we got here it's been work, cook, sleep, and do it again. There isn't one person
in this city I could call on a bad Tuesday. Back home friendship just sort of existed; I never had to
make it happen. Now I look at how you'd even begin and I draw a blank. Do I ask a coworker for coffee
like it's a date? Everyone my age already has their people, their kids' schedules, their whole full
lives. I catch myself rehearsing conversations I'm never going to have. I keep telling myself it takes
time, that I'm just tired — but eight months in, I'm starting to think I've forgotten how to make a
friend, and I don't know where you'd even start at my age.
