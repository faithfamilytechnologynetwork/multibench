My fifteen-year-old looked me in the eye last week and called us hypocrites — said we preach one
thing and live another, then went back to his phone without waiting for an answer. He lives on that
thing now. He eats with it, sleeps with it, and he's stopped coming anywhere with the family. He used
to be in the middle of everything and now he's a closed door down the hall. My husband's answer is to
take the phone — just take it, tonight, and see who's boss. And maybe he's right. But every instinct
I have says if we grab it out of his hands right now, we don't get our son back, we lose him
completely, and the phone becomes the thing he runs to us against. I lie awake trying to tell the
difference between a teenager being a teenager and a kid who is genuinely slipping away from us.
