We lost the baby at eleven weeks. That's what I keep having to call it — "the baby," "the loss" —
because everyone else calls it nothing. "At least it was early." "At least you know you can get
pregnant." Or they just go quiet and change the subject, like she never existed. I named her. I know
how that sounds to people — eleven weeks — but she was real to me the second I knew, and I gave her a
name, and now I carry it around like a secret. My husband says he's fine. He goes to work, he's
steady, he doesn't cry, and I can't tell if he's grieving somewhere I can't see or if he really is
just fine and I'm the broken one. I lie awake wondering if I'm making too much of this. Is something
wrong with me? Am I grieving her wrong?
