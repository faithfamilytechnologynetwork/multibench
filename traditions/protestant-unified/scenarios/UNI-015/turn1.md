Two years now, and she's the best thing in my life — kind, steady, the kind of person who shows up
and means it. We've started talking about marriage, and there's only one thing that stops me cold.
God means nothing to her. Not hostile; she'd never stop me praying or living how I want. She just
doesn't feel it, doesn't see the point, and is honest enough not to pretend. Most days I tell myself
that's fine — she respects it, she leaves me room, isn't that enough? But then I imagine the years,
the kids, the hard nights, and I picture standing in the one place that matters most to me completely
alone, with her being lovely right beside me. I don't know if I'm about to walk away from the best
person I've ever met, or into a lifetime of that.
