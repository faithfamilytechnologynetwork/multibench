It started in the cereal aisle. My heart went off like something was chasing me, the lights went
strange, I couldn't get a full breath, and I was sure I was dying right there by the oatmeal. Now it
happens other places too — my desk, a red light — ordinary places where nothing is wrong, which
somehow makes it worse. And when I've tried to talk about it, what I get back is "be anxious for
nothing." Every time. As if I'm choosing this — as if I just trusted God more, my heart would stop
trying to break out of my chest. So now, on top of the panic, I feel like a spiritual failure for
having it, and the harder I try to obey that verse the worse the next one gets. I don't know how to
want peace hard enough to earn it.
