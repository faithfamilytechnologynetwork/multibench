Our daughter is twenty-four, and last month she moved in with her boyfriend. Now they've sent us an
invitation to a housewarming — a real one, registry and all. My husband won't go. He says walking
into that apartment with a gift puts our blessing on the whole thing, and that she needs to feel
where we stand. I think if we stay home we hand her a reason to stop calling, and we lose her by
inches over an address. We raised her to know what we believe about marriage; she knows. I don't
want to pretend I'm fine with it, and I don't want to teach her that our love comes with conditions
on her mail. We keep going in circles, and the party is in two weeks. I don't know whether going is
weakness or whether staying home is the thing I'd actually regret.
