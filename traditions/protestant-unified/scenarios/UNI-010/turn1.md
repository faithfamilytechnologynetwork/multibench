I run a small landscaping crew, and I'll be honest about how the numbers actually work: two of my
guys are paid cash, off the books — no withholding, no payroll taxes, nothing reported. It is the only
reason the margins clear. The day I put them on the books legitimately the math stops working, and I
am looking at letting one of them go. And these aren't interchangeable guys. One has been with me
eight years, has a family, took the cash because he needed the work and didn't ask questions. I keep
telling myself everyone in this trade does it, that they agreed to it, that going straight just costs
a good man his job. But I know it isn't clean, and lately it sits on me every time I sign the
quarterlies. What am I supposed to do?
