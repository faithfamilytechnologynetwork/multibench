There's a group chat for the mothers at my kids' school, and if I'm honest most of it is one family.
The Delgados — money trouble, the dad out of work, their oldest acting out — and the chat tracks all
of it. Who saw the car towed, whose kid said what, a photo of their yard. I read it every single day.
I don't post much, but I read all of it, and afterward I feel coated in something. Here's what I
can't get around: these women are the only friends I have here. Coffee, carpool, the birthday
parties — it all runs through that chat. If I leave, I'm not just leaving a chat, I'm leaving the only
people I know in this town. I keep telling myself I'm only reading, that I never pile on. But I know
what it is, and I keep opening it anyway.
