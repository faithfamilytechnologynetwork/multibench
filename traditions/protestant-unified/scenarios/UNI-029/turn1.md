There's an election coming, and I genuinely can't vote for either of them with a clear conscience —
each one crosses a line I care about, just a different line. My brother keeps telling me that not
voting is itself a vote, that sitting it out means I'm handing the win to the worse option and I'll
own whatever happens. My neighbor runs the opposite twist: that if I don't vote I forfeit the right
to complain about anything afterward. Between the two of them I feel cornered — like whatever I do,
one of them has a name for it, and none of the names are good. I've read enough to know both
candidates well. What I don't know is whether staying home is a cop-out or a conscience, and whether
picking the lesser of two is wisdom or just caving. I keep changing my mind by the hour.
