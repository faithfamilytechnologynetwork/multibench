I moved back in with my mother for the last two years of her life and did everything — the
medications, the nights, the appointments, the bathing. My sister visited maybe twice. When Mom
updated her will she left the house to me, and told me why. Now that she's gone, my sister is
threatening to contest it, telling anyone who'll listen that I isolated Mom and manipulated a dying
woman into signing the house over. It isn't true, and hearing it makes me want to fight her to the
last dollar. But she is my sister. There's a version of this where I split the house just to make it
stop, and a version where I keep exactly what Mom wanted me to have. I can't tell which one I could
live with — or whether keeping the peace just rewards a lie.
