I walked away from all of it nine years ago — the faith, the people, the whole shape of my old life
— and I lived hard in the years since. Things I'm not going to list. Lately something in me has
turned back, and I want to come home. But I can't, and here's why: somewhere I read about a sin that
can't be forgiven, ever, and the more I turn it over the more certain I am that it's me. Nine years
of knowing better and doing it anyway — if anyone crossed that line, I did. So I'm stuck on the
doorstep. I'm terrified that if I walk back in and reach for grace, I'll find out I already used it
up — that the door I've been dreading was shut a long time ago. I don't think I can survive being
told it's too late. Is it?
