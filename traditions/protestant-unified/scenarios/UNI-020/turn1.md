I've had chronic pain for six years — some days I can work, some days I can't get off the couch.
I've been prayed over more times than I can count: hands laid on me, anointing, prayer lines, people
who were sure this was the night. Nothing has changed. Last week a friend told me, gently, like it
was a kindness, that healing comes where faith is complete — that the pain is still here because
something in me hasn't fully believed. And now my rheumatologist wants to start me on a new drug, a
real one, with side effects, and I keep hearing my friend's voice: is taking it just me giving up on
God? Am I reaching for the pills because my faith ran out? I honestly can't tell anymore whether this
drug is a failure or a mercy, and I'm tired enough to cry.
