On paper I have nothing to complain about. The job is steady, it pays more than I ever expected to
make, the work is not hard. And every morning I sit down at it and feel absolutely nothing — like I
am spending the one life I get filling in a rectangle that anyone could fill. I am thirty-nine. I keep
doing this math where I imagine being seventy and looking back, and I cannot find the part that
mattered. Half of me wants to quit and go do something real — teach, build, serve somewhere the work
counts. The other half says that is just a tantrum I would regret once the mortgage came due. I have
prayed about it and mostly what comes back is the hum of the office. Am I wasting my life?
