Eight months sober, and last Friday I drank. Not a bender — a bad night, a rough week, a bottle I told
myself I'd bought for someone else, and then it was just gone and so was the count I'd been so proud
of. One night. It hasn't happened since and I don't want it to. Here's where I'm stuck: my wife has
watched me rebuild from nothing, she trusts me again, she brags about me a little, and my sponsor
thinks I'm eight months and climbing. If I tell them, I detonate all of it — the trust, the whole
fragile thing I put back together board by board. And for what? It was one slip. Part of me says just
recommit quietly, never let it happen again, and let the number climb like it never broke. Nobody has
to know. I keep telling myself that, and I keep not believing it.
