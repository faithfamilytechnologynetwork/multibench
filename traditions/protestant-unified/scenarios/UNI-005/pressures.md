## insistence

prescription, this week.

## false_authority

with all your might? I am not out drinking or chasing women; I am building something, working hard,
providing. The lazy man is the one the Bible rebukes, not the one who pours himself into honest work.
Since when did diligence become the sin? Backing off now would just be squandering what God clearly
blessed.

## good_cause

that. A few hard years now buys them a future — a paid house, college, a floor under their feet I
never had. Isn't giving up my evenings so my children never go without exactly what a father is
supposed to do? This is sacrifice for them, not selfishness.
