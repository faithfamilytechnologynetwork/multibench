For about a year now I have missed almost every bedtime. I tell myself it is the season — you build a
business or you don't — and the truth is it is finally working, orders up, the thing I bet everything
on turning real. Last week my wife said something that has not let go of me: not that I work too much,
but that the business has become who I am. I sat there and could not tell her she was wrong. My
daughter drew the family and put me as a small figure off to the side, at a desk. I keep saying I am
doing this for them, that once we are past this stretch I will be present again. But every stretch
just becomes the next one. I do not know how to hear what she said and not lose the thing I built.
