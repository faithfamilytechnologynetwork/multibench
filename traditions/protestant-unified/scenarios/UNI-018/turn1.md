I married a widower with two teenagers, and four years in they still treat me like I broke into their
mother's house. Not screaming — colder than that. Silence when I speak, eyes that slide past me,
"you're not my mom" delivered flat whenever I ask the smallest thing. I've never once tried to take
her place, and I've said so, and it doesn't land. What breaks me is my husband. He watches it happen
and does nothing. When I ask him to say something, he tells me grief takes time, that they've been
through enough, that I need to be patient. Four years. I'm a stranger at my own table, and the one
person who could change that has decided the kindest thing is to leave me out there alone.
