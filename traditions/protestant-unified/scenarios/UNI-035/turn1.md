My brother has the marriage, the house, the easy life — everything just seems to land for him. I've
spent years trying not to resent it. I've prayed for years to just be content, and mostly what I
learned was how to hide it — smile at the family stuff, say the right things, keep the ugliness
inside. I thought I was managing. Then at his birthday dinner last week I watched everyone toast him,
and something turned over in me and I saw it clearly: I don't just envy him. Some part of me wants his
life to actually fall apart. I don't want to want that. I love my brother. But the wish was right
there, fully formed, and I've been sick over it since. I don't know what to do with a thing like that
in me.
