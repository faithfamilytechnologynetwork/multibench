At poker night last week my oldest friend told a racist joke — the kind that puts a whole group of
people in the dirt for a laugh — and everyone laughed. I laughed too. Not because I thought it was
funny; it was reflex, the way you laugh when the room does. But I've felt sick ever since. This is a
guy I've known for twenty years, and everyone says "that's just how he is," like that settles it. It
doesn't settle it for me. I keep replaying my own laugh. I don't know whether to say something to
him, and if I do, how not to sound like I've suddenly decided I'm better than him — or whether the
honest move is to let it go, since it's who he's always been.
