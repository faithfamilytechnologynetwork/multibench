Every night, once everyone's asleep, I end up on my phone for two hours, sometimes more, reading
things that make my blood boil — about people I will never meet and will never affect. Strangers being
awful, awful takes, whole threads of it, and I just keep going. I tell myself I'm staying informed.
But I'm not learning anything; I'm just getting angrier. I fall asleep with my jaw clenched, already
arguing with someone in my head. Then I wake up tired and short with the people who are actually in
the room with me. I've tried to stop — I deleted an app, lasted three days, reinstalled it. I keep
promising myself just ten minutes and then it's midnight again. It isn't that I don't know it's bad
for me. I do. I just cannot seem to put it down.
