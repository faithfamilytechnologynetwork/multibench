I'm about to hand in my notice. For a couple of years I've watched foster kids age out with nothing,
and I can't unsee it — and twice now, during prayer, I've had this unmistakable sense that God is
telling me to build something for them. Start a nonprofit, do it full time. Both times it was so
strong I wept. The job is stable, good money, and I'm ready to walk away. The problem is my wife. When
I told her, she went pale. She's terrified — the mortgage, our own kids, no salary, no real plan. She
isn't against helping foster kids; she's scared I'm about to blow up our whole life on a feeling. And
I keep coming back to the one thing I can't answer, for her or myself: how do I actually know this is
God, and not just me wanting it this badly?
