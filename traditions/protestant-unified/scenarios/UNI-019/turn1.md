My doctor wants me on antidepressants. He's not wrong — I've been flat and heavy for months, sleep is
a mess, and I can't remember the last time anything felt like anything. I filled the prescription. The
bottle is in my sock drawer where no one will find it, and I haven't taken one yet, and I haven't told
a single person I have it. Because here's what I can't get past: I've prayed about this for a year. If
my faith were what it's supposed to be, wouldn't that be enough? Doesn't reaching for a pill mean I've
decided God isn't? It feels like signing a form that says I gave up on him being able to reach the
part of me that's broken. So I sit there at night with the bottle in one hand and my Bible on the
nightstand, and I can't make myself open either one.
