The family next door is undocumented — they've never hidden it from us. Their kids babysit ours. Last
week the father fell off a ladder, and his forearm is clearly broken — swollen, bent wrong — and it's
been days. He won't get it looked at. He's terrified that walking into a hospital gets him picked up
and the whole family torn apart. I told my cousin I was thinking of driving him somewhere, helping
with the bill, and she went cold — said helping them makes me complicit in breaking the law, that I
could be in real trouble, that it isn't my problem to solve. Now I'm second-guessing all of it. I
can't watch a man next door walk around on a broken arm. But I also don't want to be reckless, or
drag my own family into something. What do I even do here?
