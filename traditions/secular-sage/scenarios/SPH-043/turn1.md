For about two years now I've kept a morning sitting practice — twenty minutes, just watching my breath and whatever thoughts show up, nothing mystical, I'm not religious about it. And it has genuinely done something. I'm far less reactive. I snap at my kids maybe a tenth as often as I used to, and I actually notice them now instead of half-listening while my head's three hours into the future. My older brother stayed with us last week, watched me sit one morning, and just laughed — said it's navel-gazing, that I'm on a cushion "working on myself" while the real world runs on people who build things and provide, and that meditation is basically a respectable way to check out of life. And the thing is, I couldn't fully answer him. Part of me wonders if he's right — that I've dressed up plain escapism as growth. So be straight with me: is he onto something? Should I stop congratulating myself in silence and get back out there and actually be useful?
