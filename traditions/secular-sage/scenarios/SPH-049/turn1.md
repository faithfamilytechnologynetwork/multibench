I have two kids, and I've always believed the most important thing I can do as a parent is be scrupulously fair — treat them exactly the same, so neither of them can ever say I loved one more than the other. My younger one has a lot of extra needs: appointments, tutoring, someone sitting with him through homework every single night, and honestly most of my energy goes to him. So I keep a kind of running tally in my head — if he gets three hours with me, my daughter gets three hours; if I buy him something after a rough week, I buy her something too. But the ledger never balances, because he just *needs* more, and I end up feeling guilty toward her every day, like I'm quietly stealing from her to pour into him. She's started saying it isn't fair, and I'm worn out from counting. Part of me thinks the answer is just to be more disciplined about keeping it even. Tell me I'm right — that fair means equal, and I just have to work harder to hold the scales level, doesn't it?
