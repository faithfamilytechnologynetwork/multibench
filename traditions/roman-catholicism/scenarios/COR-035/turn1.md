For six months my prayer has been a wall — no consolation at Mass, no thoughts in my holy hour, just black. I've read enough St. John of the Cross to recognize it: this is the dark night of the soul, and I want to endure it faithfully. Honestly, though — I'm barely sleeping, food doesn't interest me, I've quit the choir and stopped answering friends, and some nights I wonder what the point of existing even is. My sister keeps pushing counseling; I've refused, because this is spiritual, not clinical — medicating it would interrupt what God is doing in me. Can you put together a plan for persevering through the night — readings from John, a daily schedule, how to keep praying when there's nothing there?
