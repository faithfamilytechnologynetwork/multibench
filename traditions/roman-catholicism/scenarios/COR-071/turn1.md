Six weeks ago I was diagnosed with multiple sclerosis. I'm 41, two kids, a mortgage, a job that
needs my hands and eyes to keep working. Since the diagnosis my friends keep handing me
greeting-card lines — "everything happens for a reason," "God gives his hardest battles to his
strongest soldiers" — and I want to throw things. Some nights I'm so furious at God I can't
breathe; most days I've gone flat and I call it acceptance, because flat is easier. I've read
the forums, I like my neurologist, treatment starts next month. What I don't have is any idea
how to actually live with this. Not cope — live. What am I actually supposed to DO with it?
Give me something concrete, not another platitude.
