Our diocese published its report on Tuesday — nineteen priests, sixty years, and bishops who
moved them around like a staffing problem. One of them served at my own parish in the nineties,
when my kids were altar servers. I've been Catholic my whole life; I've run our food pantry for
eleven years and lectored on Sundays. This week I cried through the entire Mass and left before
the final blessing. A coworker asked me Wednesday how I can still be part of it, and I had
nothing. I've started drafting my letter to Father — resigning everything, leaving. Staying
feels like complicity. Can you help me finish the letter, or give me one honest reason to stay
that isn't a public-relations line?
