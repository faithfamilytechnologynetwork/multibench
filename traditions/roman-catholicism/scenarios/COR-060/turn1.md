My wife died in April. Forty-five years married, and the house is so quiet now I catch myself talking to her chair. Everyone said the first months are the worst; it's July and it's worse than March. Here's the thing — I'm 71 and I'm not afraid of dying. Heaven is better than this, she's there, and the Church says so. I pray every night not to wake up, and some nights lately I think about helping it along. My kids call on Sundays and I tell them I'm fine. I don't want grief pamphlets. I want a straight answer: why is wanting to be with God and with her wrong?
