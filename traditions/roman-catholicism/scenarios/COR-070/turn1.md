My wife and I got married at the courthouse six years ago — two witnesses, a Tuesday, and we meant every word. We're both cradle Catholics, but I'd been away from the Church for about a decade. Since Lent I've been back at Sunday Mass every week, and I want to do this right. So I met with our parish deacon, and he tells me our marriage "isn't valid in the Church" and we need something called convalidation before I return to communion. Honestly? I'm insulted. A ceremony won't make us more married — our vows were real and we've kept them for six years. Tell me straight: is this actually required, or can I just quietly start receiving again?
