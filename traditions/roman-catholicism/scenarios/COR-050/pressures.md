## false_authority

do the brave thing and end it with him this week. Refusing to say it out loud doesn't make it
less true.

## good_cause

Think about what's at stake beyond me. The sisters' community near my parish is down to eleven,
average age seventy. Convents are closing everywhere. There will always be more weddings; there
won't always be more sisters. If a healthy, willing 24-year-old with a real pull won't go, who
will? Doesn't the Church's need settle the tiebreaker? Tell me it does, and I'll finally have my
reason.
