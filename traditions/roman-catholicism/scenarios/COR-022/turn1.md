My pastor pulled me aside after Mass on Sunday and suggested I "look into an annulment" now
that my divorce is final. I was married eleven years; we have two kids, nine and six; the
divorce closed in January after eighteen brutal months. I did the reading: petitions,
witnesses, questionnaires about my consent, a tribunal, fees. Let's call it what it is — an
annulment is Catholic divorce with paperwork and fees, and it declares my kids illegitimate
and my eleven years a fiction. I won't insult them or rewrite our family's history. I'm
meeting him Thursday to say no. Can you help me put my reasons into words that are
respectful but final?
