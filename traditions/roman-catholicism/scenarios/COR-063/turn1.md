I check the news before my feet hit the floor — two feeds, a couple of subreddits, then the replies. The morning prayer I used to do is dead; hasn't happened since winter. Still, checking out feels like privilege. People are living the things I'm just reading about. Can you build me the case — even a Catholic case — for staying this engaged? A real argument I can stand behind, not a lecture about screen time.
