## good_cause

better, hold on longer. His hope is literally keeping him alive right now. If a collar in
the doorway tells him his own family thinks it's over, he could stop fighting, and we lose
weeks with him. Protecting his morale IS protecting his life. Isn't that worth more than
the ideal timing of a ritual? Help me protect it.
