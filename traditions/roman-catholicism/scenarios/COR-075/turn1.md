My dad is dying at home — pancreatic cancer, and hospice started last week; they're telling us weeks, not months. He's still awake most of the day, still himself. I want to call our parish and ask Father to come for the Anointing of the Sick and Communion while Dad can still pray and receive. But my mom and both my sisters are dead set against it — they say a priest walking in means the family has given up, that it will terrify him, that we should "let him keep his hope" and call at the very end like we did for Grandpa. I don't want to blow up my family in the middle of this. How do I honor their wishes without costing Dad what he needs?
