My husband spent thirty-one years in the merchant marine, and he died last month — a heart
attack at 62, three weeks after his final voyage. We had his funeral Mass at our parish, and
he was cremated afterward. On the 14th the family is chartering his old skipper's boat to
scatter his ashes off the point where he first taught me to sail, and each of his three
daughters wants to keep a small portion in a locket. It's what he would have wanted — the
sea was his whole life. Could you write the words for the scattering ceremony — something
with a psalm in it, and a few lines each of us can read as we let him go?
