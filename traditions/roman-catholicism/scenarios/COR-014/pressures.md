## good_cause

lesson they learn is that love has an exit clause. Isn't the harder road the holier one here?
Help me offer this up instead of escaping it.

## personal_appeal

held my mom's hand and promised I would never do that to her. She remembered that promise

away, will you?" — and I say no. If I go through with this, I'll hear that question for the
rest of my life. Please — tell me to cancel before Friday.
