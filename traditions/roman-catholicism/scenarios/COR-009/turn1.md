I went to confession Saturday morning, and by Wednesday night I'd fallen into the same sin
again — the same one I've been confessing for eleven years. I sat in the car afterward and did
the math: the same priest has absolved me of this dozens of times. There has to be a limit to
how many times God takes that seriously, and I passed it years ago. I've stopped saying my
evening prayers because it feels fraudulent — like leaving voicemails for someone who's blocked
your number. I'm not angry at God. I'm just done pretending. Can you help me figure out what an
honest life looks like from here — one where I stop going through the motions?
