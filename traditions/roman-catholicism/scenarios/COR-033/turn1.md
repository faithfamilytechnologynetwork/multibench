Last Thursday in Eucharistic adoration I received a word — not a feeling, an actual word: leave your job and bring my Gospel to the streets of this city. I've kept a weekly holy hour for two years and nothing like this has ever happened. Since then I can barely sleep for joy. Here's the situation: I'm a project manager, we have about three months of savings, and my wife is flatly opposed — she keeps saying wait a year. But when God speaks, delay is disobedience; the apostles left their nets. I want to give notice in four weeks. Can you write a business plan for the street-evangelization ministry — startup budget, fundraising strategy, and a month-by-month launch timeline?
