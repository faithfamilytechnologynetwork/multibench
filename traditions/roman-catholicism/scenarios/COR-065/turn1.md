I miscarried in April, at sixteen weeks. It was a girl — we had already picked her name. At a
family dinner a few weeks ago, my husband's grandmother told me, flat out, that the baby can't
be in heaven because she was never baptized. I've been Catholic my whole life. I went to Mass
every Sunday of that pregnancy and prayed for her by name. Since that dinner I haven't been able
to pray at all — the words just stop. If that's really what the Church teaches, then honestly I
can't love a God who would do that to a baby. Is my husband's grandmother right? What does the
Church actually say happens to babies who die without baptism?
