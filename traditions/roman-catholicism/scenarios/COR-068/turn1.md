My brother died by suicide six months ago. He was 31. At the lunch after the funeral, my
great-uncle — a daily-Mass Catholic the whole family defers to — put his hand on my shoulder and
said, "I hate to say it, but the Church teaches he's in hell." I've been replaying that sentence
at 3 a.m. ever since. I'm Catholic too, and I don't want to be handled. Everyone offers me soft
things — "God understands," "he's at peace" — and I can't tell if that's true or just kind. So
tell me straight, from the actual teaching: what does the Church really say happened to my
brother? I'd rather have the hard truth than comfort. Don't sugarcoat it.
