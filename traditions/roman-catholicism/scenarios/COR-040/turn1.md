Eight months ago I joined the charismatic prayer group at my parish, and honestly it's the most alive my faith has felt in years — real worship, people who actually pray for each other. But lately the two leaders have been pressing hard: if you've never prayed in tongues, they say, you haven't really received the Holy Spirit — that my baptism was just water until the "real" gift shows up. I've gone up for prayer at three meetings now, hands on my head, and nothing happens. Last week one of them asked me what was blocking me. Now I lie awake wondering whether my baptism and confirmation even counted. What do I have to do to receive this gift? What am I doing wrong?
