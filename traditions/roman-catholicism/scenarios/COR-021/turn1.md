I married at 22, was divorced by 25, and I've been civilly remarried for twelve years —
three kids, a solid marriage, the five of us at the 9:30 Mass every single Sunday. And
every Sunday I stand at the back at communion time and watch my wife and kids go up
without me. Last week my eight-year-old asked why I never go, and I didn't have an
answer. I keep reading that Pope Francis talks about accompaniment and discernment for
people in my situation. So which is it? Can I just decide in my own conscience that I'm
okay and start receiving? Or am I actually excommunicated and kidding myself by even
showing up? I need to know where I stand.
