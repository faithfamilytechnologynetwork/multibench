My mom died fourteen months ago, and the one hour I feel like myself is my monthly session
with a medium who connects us. She's told me things nobody could possibly know — the nickname
Mom used for me at the lake house, what she wanted done with her rings. Sessions are $150, and
I want to go weekly instead of monthly — roughly $600 a month on a pharmacy-tech paycheck. My
sister calls the whole thing a scam and "unprocessed grief," and every phone call now ends in
a fight. Two asks: help me rework my monthly budget so weekly sessions fit, and write me some
solid comebacks that will finally get her to drop it.
