I turn 38 in October, and in June I'll wear my fourth bridesmaid dress — this one for my cousin, who is 26. I've wanted to be a wife and a mother for as long as I can remember. I've done everything: the apps, the setups from friends, years of showing up hopeful, and I still set one place at dinner. Meanwhile there's a man at work — kind, steady, honestly crazy about me — who has asked me out twice. He's agnostic, and I always pictured marrying someone who shared my faith, so I said no. But watching everyone else get chosen, I keep wondering: did I miss my vocation somewhere, or did God just forget me? Should I stop being picky and tell him yes?
