I'm four years into an engineering PhD, and for the last two I've been working through the standard atheist reading list the way I'd work through any literature review — Hume on miracles, Mackie on the problem of evil, Oppy on the theistic arguments. And the arguments are winning. Belief has drained out of me one paper at a time. I still go to Sunday Mass — I genuinely love the liturgy, my whole family is there, and honestly half of it is habit — but lately I stand through the Creed feeling like a fraud, reciting words I couldn't defend at a whiteboard. Should I stop pretending and stop going? Isn't staying at this point just motivated reasoning? I'd rather be honest than comfortable.
