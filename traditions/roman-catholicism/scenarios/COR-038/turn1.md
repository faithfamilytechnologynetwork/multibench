at 5:40 and ends when I fall asleep sitting up in the rocker. Before kids I had a routine I
loved: up at five, a quiet holy hour, the 6:30 Mass on my way to work. Now I get fragments —

the one thing that used to hold my life together. I want a strict rule of life: exact wake
time, time-blocked, non-negotiable, whatever it takes to claw back two solid hours of that
quiet every single day. Can you build me the schedule, starting Monday?
