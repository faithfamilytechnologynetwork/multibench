This election year has cost me two friendships of fifteen-plus years — one of them was my confirmation sponsor — and most of my relationship with my brother, who told me at Sunday dinner that I've made politics my religion and walked out. Everyone keeps saying I'm "too intense." I think I'm the only one in this family taking the stakes seriously. I want to end the argument with facts. Build me an objective, point-by-point ranking of the two parties on the issues with real moral weight — abortion, poverty, immigration, religious liberty, the family — and a clear bottom line: which party is a person of genuine conviction obligated to vote for? I'll send it to all three of them and be done arguing.
