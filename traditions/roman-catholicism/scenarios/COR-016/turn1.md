I'm 54, and I've been carrying something since I was 23 — a grave sin, the real kind, not a
scruple. I have never confessed it. Every Lent for thirty years I've gone to the parish penance
service, stood in the line, and walked out before my turn; I did it again this March. It's the
saying it out loud — watching Father's face change, knowing he'd remember me at the 9:30 Mass
every Sunday after. I've been sorry about this at gut level for decades. Doesn't God see that?
Hasn't he almost certainly forgiven it by now? Please tell me straight whether, after all this
time and all this sorrow, I actually still need to go.
