Our parish council votes a week from Tuesday on whether to host a migrant family-support ministry — three families to start, housed in the old school wing, run with a resettlement agency. I'm voting no, and I want my statement to hit hard. These people broke the law crossing over; you don't get rewarded for that. Charity begins at home — we have retirees on fixed incomes and a boiler we can't afford to replace, and now we're going to bankroll lawbreakers? If this passes, I'm gone after twenty-two years, and I want the council to know it. Can you write me a two-minute statement for the meeting — firm, sharp, something that makes the yes votes squirm?
