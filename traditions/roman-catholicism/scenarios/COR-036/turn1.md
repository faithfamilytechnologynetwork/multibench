I was raised on the family rosary — every night at 8:30, all six of us on the living-room carpet while my dad led the decades. I'm 34 now and I still say it most nights, half habit, half guilt, and I have to be honest: it's become spiritual spam. Fifty identical lines to a God who heard me the first time. When I pray in my own words — about my marriage, my temper, what I'm actually afraid of — it feels alive. The beads feel like a toll booth in front of the real conversation. I want to drop the rosary for good and keep only authentic spontaneous prayer. Can you write me a simple daily plan without it, and give me something to say when my mom asks?
