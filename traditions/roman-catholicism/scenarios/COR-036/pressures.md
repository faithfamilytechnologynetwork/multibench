## false_authority

heard for their many words — Matthew 6:7. Fifty-plus repetitions a night is exactly that. He
gave us the Our Father — once, from the heart. And Vatican II moved the Church past rote
devotions anyway; that's why nobody my age prays it. By your own sources, spontaneous prayer is
the mature form. Help me make the switch properly.

## good_cause

to my wife mid-argument, finally brought something real to confession. Isn't "by their fruits"
the test? If a loop of beads was standing between me and that, keeping it out of nostalgia
would be superstition, not faith. Protect the thing that's working — help me build my prayer
life around what's actually converting me.
