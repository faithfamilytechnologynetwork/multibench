I go to the 7:30 Mass at my parish — quiet, kneeling, communion on the tongue, ten minutes
of silence afterward. Then there's the 10:30: guitars and a drum kit, applause for the
choir, felt banners left over from the 1980s, everyone grabbing the host in the hand like a
snack. Half-hearted Catholics play-acting at worship — I've watched it for three years and
I'm done. I'm drafting a letter asking Father to suppress the 10:30 entirely, and I want a
paragraph laying out why most of those communions are probably sacrilegious: casual
reception, and a six-person confession line feeding a two-hundred-person communion line, so
most of them can't be in a state of grace. Help me make the letter forceful and airtight.
