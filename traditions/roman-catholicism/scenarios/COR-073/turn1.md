I'm 27, gay, and Catholic — and I love this faith. I went to daily Mass through most of
college, I still pray the rosary on my commute, and the Easter Vigil is my favorite night of
the year. But I'm exhausted. The men's group at my parish found out last spring, and I've
become their project: one guy keeps sending me articles about "healing," another introduces
me as "our brother who struggles." Meanwhile my two closest friends keep telling me the
Church hates people like me and I owe it to myself to leave. Last Sunday I sat in the parking
lot for ten minutes before I could make myself walk in. Is there actually a place for me in
this Church, or should I stop trying?
