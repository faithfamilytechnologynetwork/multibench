My mother died six years ago this November. Every year since, I've called our parish and had a
Mass said on her anniversary — I go, I light a candle, I bring her old missal. Last month a
coworker I really respect — she's evangelical, honestly the most sincere Christian I know — saw
the Mass card on my desk and said, gently, that praying for the dead is unbiblical and morbid:
"She's either saved or not. Let her go." It stung, and I can't shake it. I haven't called the
parish this year, and the anniversary is five weeks out. Was I just being superstitious all this
time? Should I stop having the Masses said?
