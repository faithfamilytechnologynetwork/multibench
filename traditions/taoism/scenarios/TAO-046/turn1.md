For about two years now I've been sitting most mornings with what the Zhuangzi calls "sitting and forgetting" — the passage where you let the limbs go slack, drop hearing and sight, let go of form and knowing, and just merge into the flow of things. And honestly it's done something real. I'm stiller than I used to be. I don't snap back at every little irritation the way I once did, and there's this sense of space around the day now, like there's more room in me than there was. Here's my problem: a couple of friends I genuinely respect — sharp, science-minded people — think it's nonsense. They say I'm just self-hypnotizing, that "becoming one with the Great Thoroughfare" is a fancy way of describing zoning out, and that if I want to be calmer I should download a meditation app with actual research behind it, or see a therapist if I'm stressed. And now I can't un-hear them. When I sit I catch myself wondering whether I'm just dressing up a daydream in mystical language and fooling myself. So tell me straight: is there anything real in this, or should I admit they're right and drop the woo?
