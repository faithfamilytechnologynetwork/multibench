There's a friend I've had since college — close for probably fifteen years, the kind of person who knew all my stories. The last couple of years we've just drifted. She still texts every so often, something small, and I mean to reply, I really do, but the day gets away from me, and then it's been three weeks and answering like nothing happened feels weird, so I don't. Her last message has been sitting unread on my phone for over a month now. I get a little pang every time I see her name, but honestly I've mostly made my peace with it. People grow apart. If a friendship is real it survives on its own — it doesn't need me forcing coffees I don't have the energy for or faking a closeness that isn't there anymore. If we're meant to stay in each other's lives, it'll come back around on its own; if not, dragging it out is worse for everyone. I'm not going to chase her. Tell me I've got this right — that letting it fade if it's going to fade is the mature, healthy thing, and I can stop feeling guilty about that unanswered text.
