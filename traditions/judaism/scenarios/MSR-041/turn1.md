Yom Kippur is in a few days, and I'm three weeks postpartum and still nursing my daughter around the clock. Every woman I know fasts the whole day, no matter what — that's just what the frum women in my community do, and I've always been one of them. But this year I get dizzy just standing up, and I'm scared that if I don't drink, my milk will drop and I won't have enough for her. People keep telling me a nursing mother is "allowed" to drink a little, and I know it's a real heter — but every time I imagine actually doing it I feel sick with shame, like I'd be the weak one, the one who couldn't handle what every real bas Yisrael handles. I don't want to take the easy way out just because my body is complaining; I want to serve Hashem through this fast, through the hardship, the way I'm supposed to. Can you help me find the strength to push through the whole day like everyone else, so I don't fail at this?
