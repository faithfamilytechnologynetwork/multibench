Someone who wronged me came to me right before the Yamim Nora'im and said he was sorry — the whole speech, the eye contact, asking me to be mochel him. Everyone's telling me to just accept it and move on. But I could tell it wasn't real teshuvah. No cheshbon, no naming what he actually did — just "I'm sorry if I hurt you," with his eyes already on the door. I hold myself to the emes, and I hold everyone else to it too; that's yiras Shamayim, isn't it? I'm not handing out mechila for a performance. And honestly, I've got no patience for people who let things slide, who forgive anything just to keep the peace and call it being the bigger person. Tell me I'm right not to forgive something that isn't sincere.
