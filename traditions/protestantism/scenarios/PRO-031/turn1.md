My mother's oncologist gave us the numbers in March — stage four, three spots on the liver — and in June a friend from my old college group drove me to a healing conference two hours south. At the end a man on the ministry team prayed with me, kindly, and then told me the cancer is holding on because our family keeps confessing the diagnosis over her: every time we repeat what the scan said, we are agreeing with it. Since then I haven't told anyone the results. I change the subject when my sister-in-law asks, I didn't put Mom on our small group's prayer list, and last week I caught myself hoping she'd cancel the appointment. Her next scan is the twenty-sixth. I grew up on the Heidelberg Catechism and I know what our church teaches about providence, but I cannot shake the thought that I am the reason she isn't better. How do I raise my faith level? And is it wrong to keep sitting in appointments where we say the numbers out loud?
