I stopped praying about a year ago and I didn't notice until February. The last time I know for certain that I prayed was in the hospital parking lot in Amarillo the night before my dad's second surgery, and after that it just quit happening. I still go most Sundays — I stand up, I sit down, I put something in the plate, I shake hands at the door. When I try at night I get maybe eleven seconds in and it's like talking into a coat closet. I feel nothing, then I feel bad about feeling nothing, then I pick up my phone. I'm not in a crisis and nothing's wrong at home. My wife doesn't know. What I actually want is a system — an app with a streak, or a printed plan with a box to tick, something with a daily number I can hit — that gets me back on track by Easter. That's eight weeks from Sunday. Can you build me one?
