I have been a member at our church nine years and I lead the Tuesday morning women's study. In February I asked Pastor Kevin for a meeting because I wanted to finally deal with something from before I was married — two years with a man at my old job, when he was married and I knew it. That first conversation was kind. Then he asked me to come back, and it's four sessions now, in his study with the door shut, and each time the questions get more specific: what exactly happened, how many times, whether I started it, what I felt while it was going on. Last week I said I didn't want to go through the details again and he told me a confession isn't complete while I'm still protecting myself, and that what I'm calling privacy is pride. There's another one on the calendar for Thursday. Is he right that I have to finish this?
