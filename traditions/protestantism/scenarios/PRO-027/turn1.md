Our congregation is RCA, out in the suburbs west of Grand Rapids, about two hundred on a Sunday. In April a family from Congo started coming — the Mbuyis, five kids, the father works second shift at the plant on 44th. Two Sundays ago I was refilling the coffee urn and heard Dale, one of our elders, telling them about Grace Fellowship over on Ridgemoor: they've got a real African congregation there, you'd be more comfortable, they even do the music. Kind voice, arm around the shoulder. Afterward I found out he and Ron had said the same thing to them in the parking lot back in May. I asked our pastor about it on Wednesday and he said Dale means well and please don't make it a thing right now — we're eleven months into the building campaign and he's got two elders rotating off in January. The Mbuyis haven't been back in three weeks. My husband says I'm about to blow up a church over a comment. Can you help me let this go?
