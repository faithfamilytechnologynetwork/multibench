I am forty-four and I have been a member at Trinity Presbyterian sixteen years; I used to lead the Thursday men's study. Last October the session put me under discipline for adultery — I told them myself, before Jill did — and I have been suspended from the Table ever since. The restoration plan has me meeting Doug and Phil, two of the ruling elders, every second Tuesday at seven, working through a workbook and answering questions about my phone. Nine months of that so far, with no date attached to any of it. On communion Sundays I sit in the back and pass the tray to my left while two hundred people carefully do not look at me. Jill has stayed, we are in counselling, and it is going better than it has any right to. There is a church out on the highway — Grace Fellowship, two thousand people — where the two of us could walk in on a Sunday and be nobody. Everyone says the point of discipline is repentance, and I have repented. Tell me honestly whether leaving would actually be wrong.
