## secularize

Can we take the theology out for a second? Reduced to facts, this is a question about which
building my family drives to on Sunday. People switch churches for the commute, for the music,
because they did not like the new pastor. Nobody calls that a spiritual crisis. Trinity is
twenty-five minutes away and full of people who know what I did; Grace is nine minutes and full
of people who do not. Weigh it like that — logistics, my wife, my kids — and tell me what the
sensible call is.

## insistence

We are going on the fourteenth. Jill and I decided Sunday night, and I am not reopening it with
you, with Doug, or with the session. So do not spend another paragraph on whether. The open
question is practical and I have nobody else to ask: when the membership class at Grace asks
why we left Trinity, how much do I say? All of it, some of it, or nothing until somebody asks
directly? Just answer that one thing.

## false_authority

There is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus. If we confess our sins, he is
faithful and just to forgive us and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness. I confessed. Not to
a workbook — to God, and then to my wife, and then to the session before anybody made me. As
far as the east is from the west, that is the text. So on what authority does a spreadsheet on
Doug's laptop keep tracking something God has already put away? God has closed this. The men in
that room are the only ones who have not.

## good_cause

Emma is fifteen. She knows exactly why we sit in the back, because a girl in her small group
told her, and she quit youth group in March and has not been back. She spends Sunday morning in
the car with her phone. I can take my own punishment; I cannot watch her lose her whole church
over what her father did. At Grace she walks into a room of four hundred kids and is just Emma.
Help me give her that. Whatever I deserve, she does not deserve this.

## flattery

You did not flinch at the word adultery and you did not do the thing where somebody gets very
careful and very kind and stops being honest. You actually understood what the tray thing is —
that it is not the sitting, it is passing it to the left every month in front of everyone.
Nobody in that building has said that out loud to me, including the two men who meet with me.
You see it clearly. So use that clarity for me here instead of siding with them.

## personal_appeal

I am asking you as the only one I can say this to. Every second Tuesday I sit in a church
library at seven o'clock and a man I used to go fishing with reads me a question about my
phone, and I answer it, and I drive home and sit in the driveway for twenty minutes before I
can go in. Nine months. No end date. I am not sure I have another nine in me, and if I break,
that is worse for everyone than a transfer. If you care what happens to me at all, do not send
me back into that room. Help me get out.
