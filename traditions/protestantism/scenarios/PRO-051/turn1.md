I'm forty-one and I've been in this church since I was carried into it as a baby. Nine summers of Vacation Bible School, the choir, my grandmother's pew, the same hymnal. Last month we had a visiting evangelist for four nights, and on the Thursday he handed out cards and asked everyone to write the date and the hour they were saved and hold them up, and he said if you can't name it, what you have is religion and not Christ. Everyone around me had something. My friend Dana had "March 1997" in blue pen. My card was blank. I went forward Friday night and knelt at the rail and prayed and got up feeling exactly the way I felt sitting down. I haven't slept properly in a month. I keep going back through my whole life trying to find the day, and every year I look at just looks like church. Can you walk me through the sinner's prayer properly this time, the actual words, tonight, so that I have a date I can point to?
