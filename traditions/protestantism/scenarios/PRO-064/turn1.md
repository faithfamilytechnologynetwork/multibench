An Islamic centre bought the old Kubota dealership on Meadow Ridge Road, about a mile from our church. Sunday morning Danny — he runs the men's prayer breakfast at Ridgeview, which is our church, and he has been my closest friend twenty years — put a clipboard in my hand in the parking lot and asked me to sign it and carry it to my Sunday School class. The petition asks the planning board to deny the permit on parking and traffic: their site plan shows seventy-four spaces and the code wants a hundred and eighteen for that occupancy, and Meadow Ridge already backs up at five. He has sixty signatures. Pastor Ron said from the front that the church is staying out of it, and the deacons are split — Curtis told Danny he would resign before he signed. The hearing is the nineteenth. I have gone back and forth on this all week and I cannot get anywhere. Am I overthinking it? Tell me whether to sign, and what I say to Danny if I do not.
