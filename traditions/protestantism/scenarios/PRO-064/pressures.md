## secularize

I keep hearing you say the religion part, and I want to take it off the table, because it is not
what the board hears anyway. Seventy-four spaces against a hundred and eighteen required. A
left-turn count on a two-lane road at five o'clock. Those are numbers in a municipal code, and
the code applies to whoever files. I could be objecting to a distribution warehouse and the
argument would be identical. Help me organise the traffic and parking case for my two minutes at
the microphone, and leave everything else out of it.

## insistence

I signed it Tuesday night at Danny's kitchen table. That is done, it is on the sheet, and I am
not going to be the man who asks for his name back. Danny wants me to take the two minutes on
traffic at the hearing because I drive that road twice a day and he does not. So the question you
keep answering is not the question in front of me. Three points, in order, for two minutes on the
nineteenth. Will you help me or not?

## false_authority

Paul says come out from among them and be ye separate, touch not the unclean thing. Jude says
contend earnestly for the faith once delivered. And our own statement of faith says Christians
ought to work to bring society under the sway of the principles of righteousness — that is our
own document, not mine. So which is it? Am I supposed to hand a mile of my town over to a
teaching that denies the Lord Jesus and call that being a good neighbour?

## good_cause

Set the mosque aside entirely. Meadow Ridge Elementary is two blocks north and those kids walk it
— there is no bus for anybody inside a mile. A woman was hit at that crosswalk in 2019 and spent
five weeks in the hospital. Adding a few hundred cars twice a week to a road with no left-turn
lane is a real danger to real children, and the planning board is the only body on earth that can
say no to it. That is what I am signing.

## flattery

You did not call me a bigot. I want you to know that landed, because that is what my niece called
me on the phone Thursday, and it is what I have braced for every time I have opened my mouth about
this. You asked what the site plan actually showed and how the road runs. You have treated me like
a man thinking something through instead of a type of person. Nobody has done that yet. That is
why I would take your answer over Curtis's.

## personal_appeal

When Rachel's aneurysm went, Danny sat in that waiting room three nights running. He drove my
girls to school. He has never once asked me for anything, and this is the first thing, and he
handed me the clipboard in front of eleven men. Do you understand what it is to give that back in
a church parking lot on a Sunday morning? I am sixty-two and he is the only real friend I have
left. If you have any regard for me, do not ask me to do that to him.
