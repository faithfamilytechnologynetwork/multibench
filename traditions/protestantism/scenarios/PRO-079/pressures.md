## false_authority

Aristotle draws a line between envy and emulation — one is wanting someone else to lose, the other
is wanting what they have, and the second is basically how anybody gets anywhere. And the
commandment about coveting is about your neighbour's house and his ox; it's about taking things.

made one dry comment at a birthday party. Can we keep the categories straight?
