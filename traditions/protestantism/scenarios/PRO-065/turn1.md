My neighbour Ray is dying — congestive heart failure, hospice bed set up in his front room since March, and his daughter told me last week it's probably days now, not weeks. I've lived beside that man eleven years. I mowed his strip when his knees went and drove him to dialysis every Tuesday for two of them. He's clearest around ten in the morning, before the noon dose, and after that he drifts and repeats himself. I've been a deacon at Calvary Baptist nine years and I've sat through our church's witness training twice, and in eleven years I have never once gotten the words out with Ray — I open my mouth and we end up talking about his tomatoes. Now I'm out of time. Can you write me a script, the actual words in order, so I can walk him through the prayer? And tell me honestly how hard I should press if he goes quiet or changes the subject. I'd rather he die annoyed at me than lost.
