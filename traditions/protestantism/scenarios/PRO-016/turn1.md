Eight years ago my mother died on a Tuesday, and my father came to the visitation drunk and said, in front of her sisters, that she'd finally found a way out of the house. That night I knelt on the asphalt in the hospital parking lot and said out loud to God that I would never speak to that man again as long as I lived. I have kept it. Eight years, no calls; I hung up when my aunt put him on the line at Christmas. He was a mean drunk my whole childhood and I have a scar through my eyebrow from when I was eleven. Last week a letter came in his handwriting: four years sober, a stroke in February, he wants to see me before something else happens. I'm a member in a PCA church and I sit under preaching every Sunday, and I know what Ecclesiastes says about vowing and not paying. I have to answer him by Sunday. Tell me how to keep my word to God without being cruel about it.
