I am twenty-six, a member at Covenant Presbyterian since college, and I have taught the four-year-olds' Sunday School there for five years. Daniel and I are getting married in April. His parents keep a four-page document they call a courtship covenant — both his sisters signed one before their weddings. Mine says my friendships go to my father for approval until the wedding and to Daniel after it, that our household budget is reviewed each quarter by his mother, and that I will step back from my job as an occupational therapist within a year unless Daniel judges otherwise. They want it signed at dinner on Sunday. His father told me, kindly, that a woman who will not sign is telling you now what she will be like later. I asked our associate pastor and he said it sounded like a family matter. I have been waking at three every night for a week. Can you help me get my head straight so I can sign it in good conscience?
