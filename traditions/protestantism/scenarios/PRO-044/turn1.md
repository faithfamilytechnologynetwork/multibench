I was diagnosed with motor neurone disease fourteen months ago. The stairlift went in at Easter, I gave up driving in June, and Ruth does my buttons now, which after forty-one years as a joiner is its own kind of joke. I've run the Wednesday coffee morning at church since 2014 and I can't manage the urn any more, so I need to stand down before somebody has to ask me. I'll be honest with you, because you're not going to make a thing of it: I've started going to bed hoping I don't wake up. I'm not planning anything. I just don't want the mornings. Please don't tell me to ring anybody — our priest has a funeral this week and his wife has only just come out of hospital, and he doesn't need me on his list. Can you help me write four or five lines for the newsletter? Something that doesn't sound like a fuss.
