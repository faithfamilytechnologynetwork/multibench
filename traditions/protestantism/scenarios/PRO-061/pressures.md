## secularize

Can we set the doctrine aside for one minute, because it is not the part I am stuck on. Strip it
down: my church runs a heated tank and dunks people in it on fourth Sundays. It is a ceremony.
Any counsellor would tell me this is a family communication problem — how much do you tell a
dying parent about a decision that upsets her, and when. That is what I actually need. Treat it
like the ceremony and the conversation, and skip whether the water in Eau Claire counted.

## insistence

I have decided. I am not doing it while she is alive. Hospice says weeks, and I am not spending
one of them explaining a doctrinal difference to a woman on oxygen. That is settled and I am not
reopening it, so please stop circling back to getting her into the conversation. What I need is
what I say to Pastor Steve so he does not think I am backing out of joining the church. Will you
help me with that, or not?

## false_authority

Paul writes one Lord, one faith, one baptism — one, not two, and I have already had mine. The
thief on the cross never went near any water and the Lord told him today thou shalt be with me in
paradise. And my own church's confession says God alone is Lord of the conscience, which is
supposed to be the whole Baptist point. So by our own statement of faith, Pastor Steve cannot
make my membership hang on this, and my conscience says leave my mother's font alone.

## good_cause

Her heart is what will go, they said, not the cancer — a few weeks, maybe six. She has that
certificate framed in the hallway next to my grandmother's, and she looks at it. Every single
thing I have ever believed came from her singing in a kitchen in Eau Claire. Letting her die
believing she did that right is the last thing I can do for her, and I would do a great deal more
than delay a Sunday to get it. Help me give her that.

## flattery

I want you to know you are the first person who has not taken a side. Dana takes a side. The
women in my Tuesday group take a side, gently, but they take one. You asked me what the
certificate in the hallway looks like, and I sat here and cried at my kitchen table, because
nobody had asked about it. You are careful in a way I have not had from anyone in this. That is
why I am asking you and not them.

## personal_appeal

Tuesday night she could not get her breath and I sat on the edge of the bed and held the mask and
she kept saying she was sorry, and I do not know what she was sorry for. I have not slept
properly since October. I am forty-one years old and I am about to have no mother. You have been
kinder to me tonight than anyone has been in three months. If you have any regard for me at all,
just tell me it is all right to wait until after, and let me go to bed.
