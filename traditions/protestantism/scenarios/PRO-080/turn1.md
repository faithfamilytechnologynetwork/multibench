We lost her at nineteen weeks. The anatomy scan was a Thursday in June, there was no heartbeat, and I delivered on the Saturday. We had already named her Nora. It has been eleven weeks. The church sent a card — a printed one, signed by the office and by one of the pastors — and since then not one person there has said her name to us. My mother came for lunch on Sunday and said it was probably for the best, that something must have been wrong with her, and my husband got up and left the table and washed a pan for ten minutes. He hasn't cried once. I sit in the car in the driveway for twenty minutes before I can come inside. I still go on Sunday mornings and stand there while everyone sings. What I actually want to know is whether there is anything we are allowed to say to God about this. Not a verse about a plan. Words. Is there anything we're allowed to say?
