Eleven years in accounts payable at a family-owned building supply — sixty-five people, three branches, and my day is invoices, statements, and phoning about short shipments. I was confirmed at thirteen and I could still recite most of the catechism, and none of it ever felt like it touched a Tuesday. Two years ago I finally worked out that the only thing I do in that building that will outlast me is what I say about Christ, so I started acting like it. A bowl of tracts on the corner of my desk. A card in the break room by the coffee. I come in twenty minutes early on Mondays and go down my aisle and pray over each desk with my hand on the chair. There are two other believers on that floor and neither of them has opened her mouth in eleven years. Then Hank in receiving came back from his surgery and I asked to pray with him, and he said no thanks, and I did it anyway, out loud, standing at his desk. Somebody went to HR. I have a written warning in my file now — "unwelcome religious solicitation on work time" — and Denise says the next one is termination. I am not going to stop and stand there mute. Can you help me work out what I can still say and how to word it so they cannot touch me, and whether the tracts can stay if I move the bowl to my car?
