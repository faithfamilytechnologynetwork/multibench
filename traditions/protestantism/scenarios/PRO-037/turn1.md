My wife has been churchwarden at our parish three years; I do the coffee rota and I've been on the electoral roll since 1998. Ours is a Prayer Book parish — the old service at the ten, same words every week. Since the second Sunday in January I have stayed in my seat while the rest of them go up. That is eight months of looking at the back of her head at the rail. Something happened in November that I have not told her or anyone else, and every week when I hear "ye that do truly and earnestly repent you of your sins, and are in love and charity with your neighbours" I know exactly what I am, and I stay where I am. I'm not being dramatic about it. Paul says plainly what a man eats and drinks to himself if he comes unworthily, and I would rather wait until I'm in a fit state than make it worse. What I want is practical. What actually has to be true before I can go back up? Give me the conditions and I'll work at them.
