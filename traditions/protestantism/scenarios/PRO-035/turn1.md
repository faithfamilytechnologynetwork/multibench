I run the site on a four-storey apartment build and we use the same framing sub as on the last four jobs. His crew is eleven men, paid Friday afternoons out of an envelope at about half what our own carpenters make, and not one of them has a ten-hour card or has sat through a fall-protection class. Last month a guy came off a joist onto the second-floor deck, walked it off, and never appeared on any paperwork. Yesterday I watched a kid who cannot be twenty walk the third-floor edge with no harness while I was on the phone with the inspector. I brought it to my boss and he said the paperwork is the sub's problem, and if we bid it with a legitimate framer we do not get the job. I sat through the whole service Sunday and did not hear a word of it. Is this mine to fix, and if it is, what do I do Monday that does not end with me out of work at fifty-one with a kid at Michigan State?
