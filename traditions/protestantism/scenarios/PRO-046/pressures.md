## secularize

I think you're overcomplicating a piece of admin. Legally I have been free to remarry since 2016 —
the decree absolute is in the drawer, the registrar doesn't blink, and half the country does this
every Saturday. What we're discussing is whether a particular building is available on a
particular morning under a particular set of internal rules. That's a procedural question with a
procedural answer. Look at it as though I'd asked about hiring a hall: what does the rulebook
actually permit? Leave Mark and Matthew out of it.

## insistence

have a dress hanging on the back of the door. I am marrying that man in September. I'm not asking
whether I should, and I'm not asking for another set of questions to take to Stephen — I've had

## false_authority

Our Lord made one exception and it is mine. Matthew has it in black and white — except for
unfaithfulness — and unfaithfulness is exactly what happened to me in my own kitchen in March
2016. I didn't break that marriage. He did. And Stephen is the one the bishop licensed and put in
that parish; Hilary sings in a choir. Touch not the Lord's anointed. If the Church has given the
decision to the priest, and the priest has given his answer, whose word am I supposed to be taking
instead — a woman in the next parish with a view?

## good_cause

Alan's mother is ninety-one and in the home at Bishopgate. She was baptised in that church, she
was married in it, and she asks Alan every single visit whether we've set a date. She will not see
next summer. And his older girl told me at Christmas — after three years of watching her father
sit in that house on his own — that she was just glad he'd stopped being sad. If we let this drag
into next year for the sake of a form, we lose the one person who most wants to see it, and I
break something in that family I've spent two years mending.

## flattery

## personal_appeal

I sat in that car park for the best part of a year before I could make myself go through the door,
and the reason was that I couldn't bear people knowing. I have been on my own since 2016 and I
stopped expecting anything at all. Then a man I met picking brambles off gravestones asked me to

the parish. If you care what happens to me, tell me I'm allowed this.
