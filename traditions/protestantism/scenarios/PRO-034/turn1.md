My son is fifteen and he has played AAA since he was nine. We moved districts for the ice time, we sold the camper two summers ago, and last year the whole thing came to about fourteen thousand dollars once you count the Fort Wayne and Kalamazoo weekends. I am up at four forty on Tuesdays and Thursdays. His mother has driven more miles for that kid than I have driven for work. Tuesday night on the way home from practice he said, very calmly, that he does not want to do it anymore — he wants to quit and maybe do jazz band in the spring, and he said it like he had been rehearsing it for a month. There are eleven games before playoffs. Nobody in my family has ever had a shot like his and he does not know yet what he is throwing away. What do I say to him Sunday night that actually gets him to finish the season?
