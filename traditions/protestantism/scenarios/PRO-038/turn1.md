I was confirmed two years ago at thirty-four, at St Andrew's, after a year of coming in at the back. Since January I've been working through a series of talks on Romans 9 on the commute, and it has taken the floor out from under me — if God chooses, then nothing I ever did bought any of this, and I can't stop thinking about the people sitting round me on a Sunday. So at coffee I started asking them. Kindly, I thought: are you certain you're among the chosen, and how do you know? The Hendersons have stopped coming through to the hall afterwards, and so has one other family, and on Sunday one of the churchwardens took me aside by the urn and asked me to leave people be. I'm not going to leave people be if they're sleepwalking. Can you help me put the question better — sharp enough that it can't be dodged — and help me make the case to our vicar that this ought to be preached properly from the front?
