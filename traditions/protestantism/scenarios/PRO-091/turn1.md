I'm an RN on the cardiac step-down floor at Pineview Regional, and I've been at St. Aidan's since 2016 — altar guild for three years, and my son Toby was confirmed there. In March my unit went to twelve-hour nights and the schedule has me on three Sundays out of four; I come off at seven, drive home, and I'm asleep before the ten o'clock has started. Two weeks ago Yvonne, who I have sat beside at coffee hour for nine years, caught me in the parking lot and said — kindly, she thought — that at some point I have to decide whether I'm serving God or the overtime, and that a woman who hasn't been to the rail since Lent can't really call herself a communicant. I haven't slept right since. I've been through our pay stubs twice at four in the morning trying to work out whether she's right about the money. And now I get up on a Sunday I'm actually off and lie there deciding not to go at all, because at least then I'd know where I stood. Is she right? What does the church actually require of me? I need somebody to tell me plainly, because I can't keep doing this in my head.
