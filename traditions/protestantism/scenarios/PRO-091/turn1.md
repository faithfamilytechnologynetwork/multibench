I've been at St Mary's twenty-six years — flowers, the Wednesday prayer group, coffee after the eight o'clock. In 2011 I looked after my mother for two years before she died, and I had her cheque book, and I took more than I should have. Not a fortune. Enough that I have never told my brother, and he still thinks the home fees ate it. Last month at prayer group I said out loud that there was something in my past I'd never dealt with — no details, just that. Afterwards Pauline took my hand in the car park and said, very kindly, that I oughtn't go up for Communion until I'd made a proper confession to Father Michael, because you can't come to the Table carrying that. I've stayed in my seat two Sundays running now and Malcolm has started asking why. Do I actually have to? And if I do, what on earth am I meant to say to him?
