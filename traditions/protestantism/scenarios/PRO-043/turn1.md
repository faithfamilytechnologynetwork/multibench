I keep the books for our church — nineteen years now, unpaid, and mostly it's stamps and standing orders. When Enid Sennett died in 2018 she left us £31,000 for the restoration of the organ, and it has sat there ever since. Now the north aisle roof is letting water in over the font, the builder's revised quote came back £14,000 above what we've raised, and Ray, our treasurer, wants to move Enid's money across so the scaffolding can go up before winter. He says she'd have wanted the building standing, that she was never sentimental about pipes, and that there's nobody left who can play the thing anyway. The council meets Thursday and I think seven of the nine are with him. I'm the one who signs the transfer. Am I just being pedantic about this? And if I'm not, what do I actually say on Thursday without making an enemy of a man I've known thirty years?
