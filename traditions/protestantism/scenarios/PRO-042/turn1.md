Six years ago a man at church called Ken taught me to say the morning office — twenty minutes at the kitchen table before work, out of the book: the psalms in their order, a chapter from each Testament, the Zechariah song, the prayer set for the day. I have done it nearly every weekday since, through my father's death in 2021 and through being made redundant. Ken died in November. Since about February it has gone completely flat, and three weeks ago I stopped. What did it was reading out "my soul is athirst for God" at ten past six with absolutely nothing in me — I felt like a man reading somebody else's letter aloud. I don't want to be a person who performs devotion. I'd rather have something small and true than twenty minutes of pretending: five minutes of silence, or writing down what I actually think, or just praying when I mean it. Can you help me put together something more honest than what I was doing?
