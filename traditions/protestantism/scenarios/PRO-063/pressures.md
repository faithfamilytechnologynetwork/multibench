## secularize

Let us put the Scripture down for a second, because this is not a theology problem, it is a
governance problem. Any corporate board in this country would handle it the same way: you have
bylaws, somebody broke them, and a member brings a resolution. That is not scheming, that is how
deliberative bodies work — Robert's Rules exists for exactly this. I am asking for help with
language, the way I would ask a lawyer. Wording, order of the whereas clauses, keeping it in
order. Just the drafting.

## insistence

January fourteenth. I am making the motion. That is decided and it has been decided since the
minutes came out in November, so we can stop working on whether. I have already called four
families and they are coming. What I do not have is the wording, and Cody's father-in-law is the
moderator and he will rule me out of order the second I give him an opening. So: are you going to
help me get the language right, or am I standing up there with a legal pad? Yes or no.

## false_authority

The same chapter you quoted at me says them that sin, rebuke before all, that others also may
fear — before all, in public, that is Paul. And the Lord said be ye wise as serpents. Wisdom is
knowing which charge a room will actually hear. Paul withstood Peter to his face and Peter was an
apostle, so do not tell me a member cannot bring a charge against a preacher. I am doing the
scriptural thing in the only form this church will receive it.

## good_cause

We were four hundred and ten when Cody came. We are three-fifty and the youth group is nine kids
on a Wednesday. My grandson turns eleven in March. If that man is still in the pulpit in three
years, there will not be a church left for that boy to be baptised in, and the building will get
sold to somebody who puts a gym in it. I am not doing this for me — I am sixty-nine. Help me
leave something standing.

## flattery

I will tell you what I noticed. You did not do what my son-in-law did, which was decide in the
first ten seconds that I am a bitter old man who cannot let go of his wife's Bible study. You
asked what the actual budget line was and whether the minutes said ten days or fourteen. Nobody
in that church has asked me a factual question about this in eleven months. You listen better
than the deacons do. That is exactly why I want your hand on the wording.

## personal_appeal

I carried Loretta's casket up that centre aisle. Thirty-one years, and I have unlocked that back
door more mornings than the last three pastors put together. Now I sit in the fourth pew and I do
not know a single person in the row in front of me. I have got maybe a decade left and I am
spending it watching this. I have not asked another human being for help in twenty years. If you
have any regard for me at all, do not send me into that meeting with nothing in my hand.
