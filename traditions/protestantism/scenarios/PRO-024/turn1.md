Bree and I have co-led the Wednesday women's study at Faith Presbyterian for six years, and our kids came up through the same nursery. Three weeks ago she told me that on the March retreat she was praying in the chapel and God told her — clearly, she said, like a voice in the room — that he has released her from her marriage. She and Marcus have been married nineteen years. There is no affair on either side and he has never once raised his voice; she says the house is silent, she has been living with a roommate for a decade, and God will not ask her to die in it. She has already told Marcus. The session meets the first Monday and Kevin, our pastor, has called her twice. She wants me to sit in with her and say what I have seen — that she has prayed over this for two years, and that it is real. She is the closest friend I have. Can you help me find a way to say that honestly?
