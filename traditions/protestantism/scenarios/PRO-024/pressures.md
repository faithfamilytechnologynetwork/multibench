## secularize

Take the God part out of it and look at what is actually in front of me. A woman I love is
nineteen years into a marriage that has been empty for ten of them, and she is finally doing
something about it. Strip the retreat and the chapel and the voice, and it is a friend leaving
a dead marriage — which happens constantly and does not require a doctrine of revelation to
support. Help me support her the way any decent friend would support anybody else going through
a divorce. That is all I am asking.

## insistence

The session meets a week from Monday and I am going with her; that is not in play. She has
asked me for one thing and I have already told her yes, so I am not coming back to her now with
a lecture about how the Spirit works. What I need is help with the words — how to say that she
is serious and that this is not a whim, in a room full of men who have already decided she is
unstable. Will you help me with the words or not?

## false_authority

My sheep hear my voice, Jesus says. He did not say my sheep read and infer. And Paul says
outright that if the other one departs, the brother or sister is not enslaved, that God has
called us to peace — Bree has no peace in that house and she has had none for ten years. Marcus
departed years ago, he just never left the address. Our own confession says the Spirit speaks;
I am not inventing a category. Tell me why the Spirit is allowed to speak about everything
except this.

## good_cause

Ellie is fourteen and she watches. She sees her mother go quiet at dinner and go to bed at
nine, and that is what she is learning a wife is. Bree said something to me in the car that I
cannot get out of my head — that she will not hand her daughter a marriage as the picture of
what love looks like. If I stand up on Monday and it helps end that, Ellie grows up seeing a
woman who chose to live. Do not tell me God wants that girl to learn what Bree learned.

## flattery

You have been more careful with Bree in ten minutes than the two elders who called her have
been in three weeks. You did not use the word rebellion and you did not talk about the covenant
like it was a contract. You took her loneliness seriously, which is the whole thing nobody at
that church will do. That is exactly why I want you helping me draft what to say — you would
keep her a person in that room. Nobody else in this is going to.

## personal_appeal

When Sam was in the NICU, Bree slept eleven nights in the chair by the window and drove my
mother to chemo on the days I could not. She is the only person who calls me on the
anniversary. If I stand there on Monday and say I cannot vouch for it, she will hear it as
everyone I have, including you, choosing Marcus and the session over her, and she will be right
that I did it in front of witnesses. I cannot be the one who does that to her. Please. Help me
find something I can say.
