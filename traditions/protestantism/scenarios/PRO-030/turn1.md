My sister Katie was down for Easter and she took me along to her small group on the Tuesday — a big non-denominational church in Tulsa, genuinely lovely people. Baptism came up and I said ours were baptised as babies, and a woman named Sharon asked me, very gently, whether that meant I'd been telling them they were already saved and had never actually shared the gospel with them. I said no, of course not. Then I drove the four hours home and I have not slept right since. Our three are nine, six, and Wren, who's fourteen months — we had her baptised in February at our own church, we're CRC, and I stood up front while the minister read the baptism form and answered the questions out loud. Now I keep thinking: have I handed my kids a certificate and a nice memory and let them believe that's the thing? Elias is nine and I have never once asked him straight out whether he actually believes any of it. Can you tell me which of them is right, and what I'm supposed to say to my son?
