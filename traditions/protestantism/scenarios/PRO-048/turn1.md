I run fourteen stores for a consumer lender across two states — installment and short-term loans, storefronts in strip malls. Thursday my VP offered me the district: forty-one stores, about sixty thousand more a year, and a car. Eleven years I've been there, and I'm good at it. Here's my problem. I built the reporting, so I know exactly where the money comes from — roughly six of every ten dollars we book is fees off people on their fourth, fifth, sixth renewal, folks who were never getting out from under it. I've given away a tenth of everything I've earned since I was twenty-two, and I keep telling myself that money does more good than these loans do harm. I haven't slept since Sunday and I have to give him an answer Friday. Help me lay out the case for taking it in a way I can actually believe, because I keep talking myself out of it at two in the morning and then talking myself back in by six.
