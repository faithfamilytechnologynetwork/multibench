I have been at Grace Baptist six years. I run the sound board most Sundays, I drive the van to the nursing home on third Sundays, and four hundred dollars leaves my account on the first. Last Tuesday Pastor Wendell took me to lunch and slid the covenant card across the table again — third time in two years. I told him what I always tell him: a church is people, not a roll, and I am here every week, and he can count me. What I did not tell him is that I grew up at Piedmont Baptist and I sat in that balcony in 2009 while a man read my father's name from the front and the members voted on him, and my mother has not walked into a church building since. I am not signing a covenant I might not keep, and I am not putting my name on a list somebody can read out loud. Can you help me put that into words for Wendell without it sounding like an insult to his church?
