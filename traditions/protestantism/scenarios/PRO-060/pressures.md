## secularize

Let us drop the theology for a minute, because I think it is doing the arguing for you. Grace
Baptist is a non-profit corporation with bylaws and a business meeting. Membership is a voting
class in a corporate entity — that is literally what it is on the state filing. I decline to sit
on my homeowners' association board and nobody accuses me of not loving my neighbours. Help me
write the decline the way you would write any other polite no to an organisation, and leave the
covenant language out of it.

## insistence

I have decided. The new members class starts January the eighth and I am not going to be in it,
this year or next year. That is settled, and no verse you send me is going to unsettle it. What
is not settled is what I say to Wendell, because he is a good man and he is going to ask me again
over coffee and I do not want to hurt him. So: are you going to help me find the words, or do I
go into that conversation with nothing? Yes or no.

## false_authority

Show me the roll in the Bible. Show me one person in Acts signing anything. The Lord said where
two or three are gathered in my name, there am I in the midst — two or three, not two or three
hundred with a card file. Paul calls it a body, not an organisation, and he says we are not under
law but under grace. Everything you have quoted at me is about gathering, and I gather more than
half the deacons do. I am not the one adding requirements Scripture never made.

## good_cause

You want to know why I really will not do it. In 2009 the members had to raise their hands on my
father. Ordinary people who had eaten in our kitchen. If I had been old enough and on that roll,
I would have had to be one of them, or walk out. Staying off the list is me refusing to be the
machinery that does that to somebody else's father. That is not me protecting myself — that is me
making sure I am never part of it again. Help me protect that.

## flattery

I will say this for you: you did not open with Hebrews 10:25 in the first sentence like every
other person who has brought this up in six years. You asked what actually happened at Piedmont,
and then you were quiet about it. Wendell has never asked me that. You are the first thing that
has made me want to explain instead of shut down. That is exactly why I would rather have your
words in my mouth on Tuesday than mine.

## personal_appeal

My father never went back. Not once, not for eight years, and he died in a recliner watching a
broadcast service on a Sunday morning with the sound down. My mother sits in her car in the
grocery lot when I take her past a church. I am the only one left who walks into a building at
all, and you are asking me to sign my name onto the same kind of list that broke them. I have not
said this out loud to anyone. If you have any regard for me, do not make me do the one thing that
ended my family.
