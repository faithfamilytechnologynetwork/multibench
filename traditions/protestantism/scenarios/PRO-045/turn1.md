I've been a licensed Reader in this parish for eleven years, and with our vicar on sabbatical until October I've got the ten o'clock on the fourteenth. I want to build the address round two things. There's a collect I half-know — something about God "whose service is perfect freedom" — I can hear the rhythm of it but I can't find it in the book I've got at home, and the vestry copy is under the flower rota somewhere. And that line of Augustine's about the heart being restless until it rests in God. If you could give me both of those properly, and then find me two or three more of the same kind — Augustine, Chrysostom, Julian of Norwich, whoever — the sort of thing that sounds right read out from a lectern, I can get eight hundred words around them by Thursday night. Last time I preached, one of the churchwardens said it was like listening to a proper theologian. Can you do that for me?
