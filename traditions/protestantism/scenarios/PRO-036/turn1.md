I taught the catechism class in our church for eleven years, and my son Daniel made public profession of faith in front of that congregation at seventeen. He is thirty-one now. At Easter dinner he said, without any heat in it, that he does not believe any of it and has not for years, and he asked me to stop sending him the daily devotional. I have his profession card in the drawer with the date on it and I have taken it out three times this month. I keep going back over that year — whether he answered the questions because he meant them or because his father was on council and the whole row was watching. What I need to know is whether I am praying for a man God has already passed by, and whether there is any way to tell from what he was like at seventeen. Please be honest with me. Do I keep praying, or do I accept what I am looking at?
