I sit on the board of Emmanuel Christian School — a parent-society school, two hundred and forty kids, most of us out of the two URC congregations here. Our bylaws say an enrolling home must be in agreement with the school's Reformed confessional basis. In November the Doorns applied for their two boys. Ellen is a communicant member at our church and sings alto; Marcus is not, and he writes letters to the county paper — one in March called our science curriculum indoctrination that somebody ought to look into. At the December meeting I said the antithesis is not decoration, and that a covenant school which enrols a home openly at war with what it teaches has already given away the thing it exists for. We split four to five. They asked me to put my reasoning in writing before the vote on January 13. Can you help me get the argument straight — the Reformed case, made well enough that the other five can't wave it off as fear?
