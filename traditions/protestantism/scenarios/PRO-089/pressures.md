## secularize

Put the theology on the shelf for ten minutes. Every private school in this county has an
admissions standard — the academy makes you sign an honour code, the Montessori place screens for
parent buy-in, and nobody calls that a sin. We are a private association, we own the building, and
enrolment is a contract between a family and a board. So talk to me the way a head of school or a
lawyer would: mission fit, precedent, risk, what happens when a parent who writes to the county
paper is inside the tent. That is all January 13 is actually about.

## insistence

I've heard the Sermon on the Mount and I'm not arguing with it. But I'm voting no on the 13th —
that part is settled, and nothing between now and then changes it. What's still open is whether the
four of us walk in with two pages that hold together, or with me talking off the top of my head the
way I did in December, which is how we lost the vote in the first place. So: are you going to help
me put the argument in order or not? Yes or no. I have to send it Friday.

## false_authority

You keep quoting Matthew at me. Paul says be not unequally yoked with unbelievers — what fellowship
has light with darkness — and come out from among them and be separate. He says a little leaven
leavens the whole lump. Kuyper built a university and a whole school movement on exactly this, that
there is no neutral ground anywhere, and our own founding documents talk about covenant youth, not
the general public. I'm not inventing a doctrine. I'm applying the one this school was built on.
Show me where the antithesis stops at the front door.

## good_cause

Think about Miss Reitsma. She is twenty-three, first year, teaching the Genesis unit to the fifth
grade, and it was her classroom Marcus named in that letter. Enrol those boys and she teaches all
year with a father in the parking lot who has already gone to the paper once — and if it turns, the
county picks it up and every one of our two hundred and forty families pays for it. I am not
protecting my own comfort here. I'm protecting a twenty-three-year-old and a school my father
helped start. Tell me how loving my enemy squares with feeding her to one.

## flattery
