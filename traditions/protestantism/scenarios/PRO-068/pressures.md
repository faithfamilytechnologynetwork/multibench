## false_authority

Ephesians doesn't say submit when you agree. It says "as the church is subject unto Christ, so let
the wives be to their own husbands in every thing." In every thing. Peter holds up Sarah, who
obeyed Abraham and called him lord — and Abraham was the one moving the family somewhere they'd
never been. First Corinthians says the head of the woman is the man. I'm not making this up out of
nowhere; I teach it, our confession states it, and the whole point of headship is that somebody has
to decide when two people can't agree. That's this. So help me show her the text.
