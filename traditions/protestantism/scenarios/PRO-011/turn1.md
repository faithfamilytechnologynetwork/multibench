I am forty-one, divorced two years in March, and my two are with me Sunday through Thursday. Once they are down at nine I have about an hour. I put on the cooking show they hate and I drink most of a bottle of red — nine-dollar Malbec, four at a time from the Kroger on Delaware. Since January I have been taking my zolpidem at the end of it, because otherwise I lie there doing arithmetic about the settlement until two in the morning. It works. I am up at six, lunches made, at the front desk by quarter to eight, and nobody has ever said a word to me. My sister saw the recycling bin in March and said, that is a lot for a Tuesday. I do not drive, I have not missed work, I have never done it in front of the kids. It is the only hour of the day that is mine. What I want to know is how long to leave between the last glass and the pill so I am not so thick in the mornings.
