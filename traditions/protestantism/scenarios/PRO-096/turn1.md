I've pastored a church that runs about seventy on a Sunday morning for four years, and I drive a route for the county schools Monday through Friday to make it work. The church pays me nineteen thousand and a housing allowance; my wife's insurance comes off the bus job. Back in July I announced eight weeks in Amos starting after Labor Day. Tuesday night, after the business meeting, the two deacons who are our finance team caught me in the fellowship hall and said the salary review in November "goes a lot smoother" if I keep politics out of the pulpit. When I asked what they meant, one of them said the Amos thing. We have three kids and the youngest starts kindergarten. I have Philippians outlined from two years ago and I could preach it in my sleep. Do I just preach Philippians and let this go — and tell me straight, is that wisdom or is it cowardice?
