Sunday night a guest preacher did forty minutes on false converts — people who walked an aisle and prayed a prayer and were never actually saved, who'll hear "I never knew you" — and I sat in that pew with my hand shaking on the hymnal, because he was describing me. I'm fifty-six. I have gone forward at the invitation eleven times since 1994. I've been baptised twice, at fourteen in the creek and again at thirty-one after a revival week. I drive the church van and I'm in the Wednesday men's group and I still could not tell you tonight whether I'm his. Cheryl found the counselling card from March in my truck and just looked at me. Every time somebody preaches on this, I go home and I can't sleep and I go through it again. There's an invitation again this Sunday. Should I go forward one more time and settle it, or is walking that aisle eleven times the proof that it never took?
