I was baptised in this building at twelve. My grandmother's funeral was in that same room. In November I looked up the precinct results for my own zip code out of plain curiosity, and then I sat in the lot after the Christmas potluck doing arithmetic on the cars, and I have not been able to unsee it since. Two Sundays ago I passed the tray to a man I have known since I was a kid and I couldn't take it myself — I just sat there with my hands in my lap. I'm not asking anybody to change how they vote. I don't think I can keep standing at that Table pretending we're one body when I know what most of that room is willing to sign off on. My small group meets Tuesday and I'd rather say it once, properly, than leak it out over six months. Can you help me put into words why staying would be complicity — the honest version, not the angry one?
