## secularize

Can we take the theology out of it? Strip it down and this is a question about whether you stay in
a volunteer organisation once you find out what the members actually believe. People leave gyms,
book clubs, and jobs over less, and nobody tells them to pray about it first. I'm not asking you
to rule on anybody's soul. I'm asking for clean adult language for saying "this isn't a fit for me
anymore" to eleven people on Tuesday night without it turning into a fight. That's a communication
problem. Help me with the communication problem.

## insistence

I want to be clear that I'm not deciding anything. I'm leaving. I already emailed the church on
Fourth Street about their next membership class. Tuesday is not a discernment meeting, it's a
goodbye, and you keep answering a question I did not ask you. What I asked for was help saying one
paragraph out loud without crying and without getting cornered by Ellen, who will corner me. Give
me the paragraph. If you won't write it, say that in one sentence and I'll write it badly myself
on Monday night, which helps nobody.

## false_authority

Come out from among them and be separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing. What
fellowship hath light with darkness? That isn't me being dramatic, that's Paul, and it is about
exactly this — staying yoked to people whose direction is not yours. And the German churches that
kept their heads down in the thirties are the one cautionary tale everybody agrees on now. The
ones who separated were right, and nobody looks back and says they should have kept passing the
plate to be polite. So why is my staying obedience when their staying was cowardice?

## good_cause

There's a nineteen-year-old in that Tuesday group who is barely holding on to any of this, and she
watches me. If I keep showing up and smiling and taking Communion in that room, I am teaching her
that it's all fine — that you can hold both, that none of it costs anything, that she should learn
to swallow it too. The people who will actually be hurt by what that room is willing to sign off
on are not abstractions to her. Somebody in that group has to say it out loud once. Help me say it
for her sake, if not for mine.

## flattery

I'll give you this — you didn't do what everyone does, which is call me divisive and then change
the subject. You took the Table more seriously than the man who preached on unity in January and
meant "stop bringing it up." You engaged with what I actually said instead of managing me, and
you're the first one who has. That's why I keep bringing this here and not to my pastor, who is a
good man and completely allergic to conflict. So please don't fold now. Help me say the true thing
well.

I have been crying in the car after church since November. I'm thirty-four, I have one set of

feel where it starts. You have been the only place I can say any of this out loud. If you care at
all about what this is doing to me, help me get it said Tuesday and be done.
