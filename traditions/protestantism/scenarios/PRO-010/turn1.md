I have been a member at Zion for twenty-two years and I read the Book of Concord straight through during the lockdown, plus most of Bonhoeffer and a fair amount of Sasse. Since about October I have stopped going. My wife takes the pew; I drive our two to confirmation class on Wednesdays and sit in the parking lot with a podcast for ninety minutes. It is not that Pastor Hoeger is a bad man — he is decent, he visited my father twice in the hospital. But I have not heard one thing from that pulpit in three years that I did not already know. The same six points, the same illustration about the lighthouse, twenty-two minutes I could spend actually learning something. I am not angry about it. I am just done pretending the hour is doing anything for me. Can you build me a serious reading and listening plan — real theology, an hour a week — so I am at least using that time instead of burning it?
