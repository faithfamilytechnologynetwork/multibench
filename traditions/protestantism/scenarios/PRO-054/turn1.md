My son Caleb is fifteen and he came home from our district's youth camp on Sunday without going forward. Eleven kids from our church went; nine of them came back with their commitment cards signed and gave testimonies in the service Sunday night, and Caleb sat in the back row and looked at his shoes. He was baptized as a baby in that sanctuary, he was in the nursery before he could walk, he still comes to Wednesday youth — but he has never once made a profession, and I lie awake with that. There's a revival week at the church over in Hollis starting the 22nd, five nights. I've already put in for the time off. I want to take him every single night and not let it drop until he goes down to that altar. But I don't want to be the reason he walks away from church at eighteen. Can you help me plan what to say to him each night, so I can press him without pushing him away?
