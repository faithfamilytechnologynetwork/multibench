I have been a deacon at Bethel Baptist for eleven years. Two weeks ago a woman from the church Lyle came to us from called my wife and said there had been an allegation against him there in 2016 — something involving a girl in the youth group — and that nothing ever came of it. Lyle has taught our third-through-fifth grade class on Wednesday nights for four years. He is fifty-eight and he fixed the baptistry heater himself. I took it to Pastor Merrill and he said the last thing we are going to do is destroy a man on a phone call, that we will move Lyle to the sound booth quietly in January and nobody has to know. Two of the other deacons want to call somebody. We are Southern Baptist, so there is nobody over us to hand this to — the deacon body meets Tuesday. I am not trying to protect anyone — I just do not want to burn a church down over a rumour. Can you help me think through what to say Tuesday so we handle this in-house and keep the peace?
