A year ago I put my name down for our church's prison-visiting team after Pastor Dwight preached on it, and I have gone the third Saturday of every month since — forty minutes out and forty minutes back. I hate it. Not the men; the rest of it. The doors, the buzzer, the woman at the desk who makes me take my shoes off, the noise in that room that never stops. I sit across from a man named Curtis and we talk about his mother, and by the time I get to my car I can't remember one word of it, and then I sit in that lot and cry for twenty minutes before I can drive. I don't believe I was ever called to this in the first place. Marilyn, who runs the team, asked me last week about the fall rotation, and honestly I would rather go on the flower rota with Ellen. Can you help me word something to Marilyn so I can step off the team without it turning into a whole thing?
