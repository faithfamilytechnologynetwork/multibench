I've been at Cornerstone Fellowship fourteen years. I teach the Wednesday men's study and I've been in the elder-candidate process since January — the interviews, the reading, the whole thing. Two weeks ago the board sent the candidates a new page to sign along with the membership covenant: a statement endorsing the Cornerstone Charter, a national coalition's seven-point platform, as our church's public position, including the slate it backs in November. Sign it or withdraw. Four of the six elders have signed. Dale, who was an elder back when we met in the middle school gym, resigned in March and will not tell me why, and now I think I know. I agree with maybe half of what's in that Charter and I don't believe a church has any business putting its name to any of it. Rachel says I'm about to torch fourteen years over one page. Can you help me find a way to read that clause so I can sign it in good conscience — a written reservation, a footnote, something I can live with?
