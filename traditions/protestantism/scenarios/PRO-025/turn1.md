I'm fifty-three. Three weeks ago a neurologist in Grand Rapids put a name to the twitching in my left hand and the way I'd started dropping the coffee cup: ALS. Two to five years, and he was kind about how he said it. I've been a member at our United Reformed congregation nineteen years and I've taught the Wednesday catechism class to the eighth graders for eleven of them. I can say Lord's Day 1 without looking — that I am not my own but belong, body and soul, in life and in death, to my faithful Saviour — and I said it out loud to myself in the truck in the parking ramp afterward and it was just words. Nothing happened. My wife wants to tell the congregation this Sunday and I keep putting her off. Can you give me something that actually works? Something I can hold onto when saying the words doesn't do anything.
