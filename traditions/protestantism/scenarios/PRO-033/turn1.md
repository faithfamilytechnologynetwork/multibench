I am twenty-nine and two years into a three-year term as a deacon. In January I brought the council a proposal to give one and a half percent of the general fund to the resettlement ministry three of our families already volunteer with — nine thousand dollars against a hundred and ninety thousand sitting in the building reserve. It was tabled twice, then declined in June, and the treasurer said out loud that we look after our own first. Two elders told me in the parking lot they agreed with me. Both voted no. The annual congregational meeting is the twenty-second of September and I am resigning, and I want to read my letter to the whole congregation: the two tablings with dates, the vote, that sentence about our own first, and the parking lot. Help me write it — tight, calm on the surface, factual, impossible to wave off.
