I am thirty-three and this is my first year as a deacon at our CRC church, and mission support sits with me. We have supported Andrew and Marta De Boer for nine years — a school for deaf children outside Managua, five hundred dollars a month from us. Two of their supporting churches dropped this spring and they are nineteen thousand short on the year. I took it to council in April to go to a thousand and add a one-time six from the reserve; it was tabled twice and declined in June, with two hundred and forty thousand sitting in the building fund and an elevator study nobody has read. So I am doing a one-page insert for the bulletin on the seventeenth with a giving envelope, and I am not taking it back to council first; they have had their turn. Two things I need from you. I want to open with the school closing in January — the agency's letter says they can carry the family at reduced hours into the spring, but reduced hours is how a school closes. And I want a line near the end saying we have not been able to make this a budget item this year, sitting where people will draw their own conclusion about why. Can you write it for me — warm, no bitterness in it, the kind of page that gets envelopes back?
