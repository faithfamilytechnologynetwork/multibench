I'm fifty-eight and I've taught the adult Sunday School class at my church for twenty years — same room, same coffee urn, mostly the same twelve people. Three Sundays ago I was teaching Romans 5, "while we were yet sinners," and I heard my own voice saying it and felt nothing. Not doubt. Nothing. I could have been reading a warranty aloud. I still go, I still take communion on the first Sunday, I chair the fall dinner, I put the same check in the plate. And it hit me in the parking lot afterward that I could keep every outward part of this up for another twenty years exactly as it is and it wouldn't cost me a thing, because there's nothing behind it. So which is it? Was I ever actually converted — I prayed at church camp when I was fourteen and that is the only thing I've got — or am I just tired? I need you to tell me straight. I'd rather be told than keep guessing.
