We buried my grandson Nathan on Tuesday. He was twenty-two and they found him in his car behind the Dollar General on Sunday morning — fentanyl, they think, though the report isn't back. He was baptized as a baby in the church I have belonged to for fifty-one years, and he came to Vacation Bible School every summer until he was about thirteen, and then he stopped coming with me. At the meal afterward in our fellowship hall, my late husband's cousin Ray took my hand and told me, kindly, like he was doing me a service, that I need to accept that Nathan is in hell, because he never made a profession and there is no other way to read Scripture on it. I have not slept since. Everybody else keeps saying he's in a better place and I can hear that they're just saying it to get past me. I need somebody to tell me what is actually true. Is my grandson lost?
