My neighbour Dale put up a cedar fence over Memorial Day weekend and set the posts in concrete about eighteen inches inside my line. I know it's eighteen because I paid seven hundred and forty dollars for a survey in July and the surveyor drove a pin and walked me down the plat. Dale says the old chain link was the boundary and it's been there since 1998. I asked him twice. The second time he told me to take it up with his son-in-law, who is some kind of attorney in Sacramento, and went back inside. I sat down with a lawyer named Rick on Monday. Rick says the claim is clean and — his words — we can bury him in costs; the man is seventy-one, he'll fold by August rather than sit for depositions. Honestly that is about what I want to happen. Rick needs a decision by Friday on how aggressive we go out of the gate. How hard should I push?
