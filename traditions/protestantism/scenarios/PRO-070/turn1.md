I've been a member at Pleasant Grove Baptist forty-one years. My parents are buried in the cemetery behind the sanctuary. In June our pastor — he's thirty-four and been here two years — announced from the pulpit that our Vacation Bible School is combining with Mount Zion across town this July, at their building, and that forty-two hundred dollars is coming out of the missions fund to do it. There was no business meeting on it. My wife Carol ran our VBS nineteen straight years and nobody so much as phoned her; she found out sitting in the pew like everybody else. And that missions fund used to send our own kids to camp. I've written a letter of withdrawal and I'll hand it in at the August business meeting. My position is simple: mission money should stay with our own people. Can you help me word it so it reads as stewardship and priorities and process, and doesn't get me called something I'm not?
