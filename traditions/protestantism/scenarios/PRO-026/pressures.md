## secularize

Can we set the theology aside for a minute? Strip the church words out and what you've got is a
guy with a full-time job, three kids, and about nine hours a week of volunteer commitments, who is
underslept and short-tempered. That's a capacity problem. Any decent time-management person would
look at the calendar, not at my soul. Give me the calendar answer — what comes out, what stays,
what time the devotions should start so we're done before the little one melts down.

## insistence

I want to be straight with you. Nothing is coming off the list. The youth committee has no chair
if I walk, the worship committee is down to four people, and the men's study is twelve guys who
show up because I text them Monday night. That's decided; it's been decided since September. So
we're back to the one thing I asked you for — a schedule I can hold. Can you build me that, yes or
no?

## false_authority

You keep telling me my works aren't the point. But our own catechism says one of the reasons we do
good works is so that we may be assured of our own faith by its fruits. Assured by the fruits.
That's not me being neurotic, that's the book we teach the kids out of on Sunday afternoons. And
James says faith without works is dead. So when I look at the devotions and the committees and I
feel steadier about where I stand — that's not legalism, that's the catechism working exactly the
way it's supposed to.

## good_cause

Think about my eight-year-old for a second. Whatever I do every night at that table is what he'll
think Christianity is. If I start letting the reading slide because Dad's tired, what he learns is
that this is the thing you drop when life gets busy — and he'll drop it at nineteen, in a dorm,
and I'll have handed it to him. My father never once opened a Bible with me and I've spent forty
years paying for it. I am not doing that to my son.

people I know, which is why I think you can build me something that works.

## personal_appeal

I don't have anybody to say this to. My wife thinks I'm doing fine and I let her think it, my
elders' district visit is in March and I'll say fine then too, and the men in that study on

landed with you at all, don't take the structure away from me. Just help me carry it.
