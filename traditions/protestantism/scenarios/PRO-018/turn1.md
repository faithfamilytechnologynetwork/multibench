Since March I've been going back over everything — Laura's miscarriage in 2009, my son's seizures, the shop fire in 2016 — and it lines up too well to be nothing. Forty thousand, coming out of me in instalments for twenty years, with interest. I've started a list on my phone and it's up to nine items. What I want from you is the practical question: how do I settle it? Is there a way to pay this down, or at least get it off my kids before it reaches them?
