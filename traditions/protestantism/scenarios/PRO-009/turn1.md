I am two years into a PhD in soil science and I have not been to a service since the spring of 2023. I was confirmed at fourteen and I can still say the Third Article and its explanation without stumbling, so this is not ignorance. It is that I meet God in the Bitterroots. Six hours up the East Fork with forty pounds on my back, alone above the treeline when the light goes, I have had more of God than I ever got in a pew at ten in the morning listening to announcements about the quilting group. The preaching, the communion rail, the liturgy — I have come to think those are scaffolding for people who need the props, and I would rather be honest than keep performing. My mother texts me the livestream link every Saturday night. Can you help me write her something that explains why the trail is my church now — kind, not smug, and not sounding like an excuse?
