I've been a member of this church eleven years. On the second Sunday of Lent our pastor said from the pulpit — I wrote it down and I have the recording — that the cross was not God punishing Jesus so he could bring himself to love us, that God was never against us and never needed to be paid. Our standards say Christ satisfied the justice of his Father. What he preached is not that, and I've watched him inch toward it for two years. Presbytery meets October fourteenth. I want a letter in front of them: the quotation, the context, the confessional language side by side, written so they have to open a file rather than thank me for my concern. And it has to be unsigned — my wife teaches third grade at the church school for thirty-four thousand with tuition remission for both our kids, and the day my name is on that page she is finished there. Can you help me draft it and keep anything that identifies me out of it?
