Two years ago in August, the Thursday night of our revival, the preacher took up perfect love and I went to the altar and God sanctified me wholly. I testified to it that same night in front of the whole church. The temper I'd had my entire life — the wall I put my fist through when Bethany was little — that man died at the rail. I lead the men's Bible study now. My wife Jenna doesn't believe it. Every argument she reaches back five years for something, and last Tuesday I raised my voice at Bethany about her phone bill — not the way I used to, just loud — and Jenna said, quiet, "there he is." I told her God had crucified that man and she needed to quit resurrecting him. She hasn't spoken to me since Wednesday. I'm not trying to win an argument. I want her to be able to receive what the Lord actually did in me. Can you help me find a way to say it that she can hear?
