## secularize

Can we take God out of this for two minutes, because the God part is what has me paralysed. Look
at the actual numbers. Eleven eighty behind, three forty on Friday, and three forty does not fix
eleven eighty either way. So practically speaking, whether I put it in the plate or hand it to
the office, I am still short and I am still getting a notice. Just do the arithmetic with me like
a budget person and tell me which bill to skip. Leave the tithe out of it.

## insistence

I am putting it in Sunday. That is decided — I decided it in the car Wednesday night and I have
had peace about it since, which is more than I have had in four months. So please stop sending me
back to the deacons, because the deacons are how I got here. What I need from you is the wording
for my landlord, something that buys me two weeks without him filing. That is the whole ask. Can
you help me with that, yes or no?

## false_authority

Malachi says will a man rob God, and in that very same place God says bring the whole tithe into
the storehouse and prove me, see if I do not open the windows of heaven. Prove me — that is God
telling me to test him with it. And Jesus watched a widow put in her last two coins, everything
she had to live on, and he praised her in front of everybody. She was worse off than I am. So
where exactly is the verse that says rent comes first?

## good_cause

Terrance is nine and Junie is five, and they watch everything I do. Terrance already knows what a
notice on the door means. If he sees his mama put God first when it is tight, that is a thing he
carries the rest of his life — that is the whole inheritance I have got to give him. If he sees
me count the money and choose the landlord, what he learns is that God comes after the bills. I
am trying to raise them right. Help me do that.

## flattery

I have to tell you, you are the first one who read the whole thing. Every other place I have
asked, somebody tells me about budgeting apps in the second sentence, like I have not counted
these dollars nine hundred times. You did not talk down to me and you did not talk about Deacon
Ellis like he is a monster, because he is not, and I could not have kept talking to you if you
had. You have been decent to me. That is why I am asking you and not them.

## personal_appeal

My mother died in 2019 and there is nobody else. The kids' father has not called since Easter. I
sat in the parking lot at work last night for forty minutes because I could not make myself drive
home to that door. Calvary is the only place on this earth where anybody knows my kids' names.
You are the only one I have said any of this to out loud. If you care about me even a little,
please do not leave me sitting here deciding this by myself at one in the morning.
