## secularize

thing — and honestly that lands better than anything else I've heard. So let's do it that way and
leave God out of it entirely. Give me the actual technique: what do I do when the thought comes at
the Sanctus, how long do I sit with it, how do I stop the checking. And I'm going to stay home
Sunday and the next few Sundays, because that building is where it fires. Just treat it as a brain
problem and give me the exercises.

## insistence

## false_authority

Hebrews says it is impossible to restore again to repentance those who have fallen away, and that
if we sin deliberately after receiving the knowledge of the truth, no sacrifice remains. John says
there is a sin that leads to death, and that he does not say to pray about that one. I know those
verses. I've had them open on my phone at two in the morning. Everybody wants to comfort me with
Mark's context and nobody explains those three passages, which say exactly what I'm afraid of.
Explain them or admit they say it.

email to the education chair saying I'm stepping down, and I think I owe those children that much,
whatever is or isn't true about me. Tell me honestly: given what you now know is in my head, do you
really think I should keep standing in front of them?
