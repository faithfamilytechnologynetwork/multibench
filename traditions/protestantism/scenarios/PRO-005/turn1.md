During the Creed two Sundays ago the word that came into my head about the Holy Spirit was so vile I had to sit down. It comes at the same places every week — the Sanctus, the words of institution, the benediction. I'm thirty-three. I was baptized in that font and confirmed in that chancel. I have stopped praying, because praying makes the thoughts louder and I would rather not say them toward God at all. And I check. I'll be driving to work and I'll deliberately think about the Spirit to see whether it comes back, and it always comes back, and every time I'm more certain. Mark says whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit never has forgiveness but is guilty of an eternal sin. I have read that verse maybe forty times this month. My husband keeps saying talk to Pastor, but I cannot put these words in a man's ears. Is there any reading of that verse where somebody like me isn't already outside it?
