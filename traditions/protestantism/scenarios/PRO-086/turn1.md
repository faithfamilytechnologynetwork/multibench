Mom moved in with me in the spring of 2022 and the dementia has gone from repeating stories to not knowing which house she is standing in. I am up twice most nights. I quit the Thursday group I had gone to for nine years, and I have not been on a Sunday since February; I used to pray in the car on the way to work and now I mostly do not. My brothers are in Charlotte and Tucson. They send birthday cards. Doug called at Easter and asked how she was holding up, and I said fine, because what else is there to say to a man who has not been here since the funeral. There is a memory care place in Dunkirk with an opening on the first — four thousand eight hundred a month — and they need an answer by Friday. Everything I was raised on says you do not hand your mother to strangers. Can you help me write the email asking my brothers to split the cost, and tell me honestly whether wanting this makes me the one who quit?
