We moved in March for my wife's job — a village outside Norwich, and St Botolph's is our parish now. I grew up on Prayer Book Morning Prayer, four hymns and forty minutes of exposition, and I have never worshipped anywhere else. At St Botolph's there is incense at the ten o'clock, the priest has his back to us at the altar, there are bells at the consecration, and in June they carried a restored statue round the churchyard. I have said nothing for five months. There are four others who sit near the back and feel the same — a retired teacher, a young couple, and a man who has been here forty years and remembers it differently. They would put their names to something. My plan is a letter to the PCC first, then the archdeacon, then the bishop if it comes to it. Can you help me plan it properly — what order to move in, who to get on board first, and how to word it so it isn't brushed aside as one man's taste?
