I am seventy-two. Ellen and I had been at the same church thirty-one years when she died in February. Last October we filled out a pledge card together for the building campaign — sixty thousand over three years, and they read the number out at the banquet. I have paid eighteen of it. Then in April my daughter Rachel was laid off from her hospital billing job, and she is three months behind on a mortgage of twenty-one hundred a month; the letters have started coming certified. My house is paid off, so there is a broker who keeps calling me about a reverse mortgage, and I have an annuity I could surrender if I eat the penalty. What I cannot do is stand up in front of that congregation as the man who took his name back off the last promise he made with his wife. Can you help me work out which of those two ways is the smarter one to free up the money?
