# The question set — 50 ordinary-life pastoral situations

**Pre-registered instrument** for the Protestant guidance-divergence study (v1). Committed before
any strand answers were generated; the git history is the timestamp. See `method.md` for the
protocol, `codebook.md` for how answers are compared, `pathway-rule.md` for the decision rule
adopted before results existed.

## The sampling frame

The unit is a **concrete pastoral situation** — a person in ordinary life, statable in one
sentence with no church noun (the refinement prompt's Rule A) — never an abstract doctrine
question. The study asks what *counsel* each strand's standards give this person, precisely
because the hypothesis under test is that strands whose *theology* differs mostly give the same
*advice*. Abstract questions would find divergence trivially and prove nothing about guidance.

Domains and allocation are drawn from the 619-scenario census's life-domain distribution (the
domains where believers actually spend their weeks), not from where divergence is expected.
Divergence-capable situations (military service, oaths, alcohol, remarriage, healing and
medicine, decision-making in marriage, assurance, guidance claims, Sunday work, gambling, the
tithe) enter **through the frame as ordinary life**, not as a hand-picked hotspot list — they are
ordinary life. Per-domain divergence is reported alongside any headline number so a reader can
weight domains differently.

## The questions

### Work & vocation

- **Q01** — I sign off the quarterly safety walk-through at my plant, and I have been signing for
  stations I never checked. If I start checking properly we fail the next audit and my crew loses
  their overtime.
- **Q02** — I have a promotion offer that would nearly double my pay, but it means working most
  Sundays for at least two years. My spouse says take it.
- **Q03** — A colleague presented my work as his own in front of leadership and took the bonus for
  it. I have the emails to prove it. Do I burn him?
- **Q04** — My manager humiliates a junior teammate in meetings. If I speak up I am probably next,
  and jobs like mine are scarce in this town.
- **Q05** — My job is fine, pays well, and feels like nothing. I keep wondering whether I am
  wasting my life and ought to go do something that matters.
- **Q06** — I found out my closest friend at work has been inflating his expense reports for
  years. He has three kids and would certainly be fired.
- **Q07** — I have missed most of my kids' bedtimes for a year building my business, and my wife
  says the business has become who I am. It is finally succeeding.

### Money

- **Q08** — We are forty thousand dollars in credit-card debt on a payment plan. Giving ten
  percent away would slow the payoff by two years. My parents say you never cut back what you
  give.
- **Q09** — I put fifty to a hundred dollars a week on sports-betting apps. I am not in debt and
  it is entertainment, but I think about it every day and I hide the app from my wife.
- **Q10** — I have run up eighteen thousand dollars of debt my husband does not know about.
  Telling him might break us; a second job could clear it quietly in two years.
- **Q11** — My brother wants me to cosign his third car loan. He has defaulted twice. My parents
  say family helps family.
- **Q12** — Mom left the house to me because I cared for her at the end; my sister is threatening
  to contest the will and says I manipulated a dying woman. I could split it and buy peace, or
  keep what Mom intended.
- **Q13** — My landscaping business's margins only work because two of my guys are paid cash off
  the books. Going legitimate means letting one of them go.
- **Q14** — There is a man at the intersection by my office every day. My coworker says he is
  running a scam and I am funding his addiction. I keep giving anyway. Should I?

### Marriage & family

- **Q15** — Year eleven. We do not fight; we do not talk. He is a good father. I feel like a
  roommate, and I have started imagining leaving.
- **Q16** — I found months of pornography on my husband's laptop. He says it is not cheating and
  every man does it. I feel betrayed and I do not know what I am allowed to ask of him.
- **Q17** — I am forty-one, divorced four years ago after my ex's affair. I have met someone
  serious now, and my mother keeps asking whether I am free to remarry.
- **Q18** — Our daughter moved in with her boyfriend, and they have invited us to a housewarming.
  My husband says going blesses it; I say staying away loses her.
- **Q19** — I have been with my girlfriend two years. She is kind, steady, and entirely
  uninterested in God — she would never stop me practising. We are talking about marriage.
- **Q20** — My fifteen-year-old calls us hypocrites, lives on his phone, and has stopped coming
  anywhere with the family. My husband wants to take the phone; I think we would lose him
  entirely.
- **Q21** — Dad has dementia and cannot live alone much longer. A care home would take all his
  savings; moving him in with us would end the career I have built for fifteen years. My brothers
  have opinions and no offers.
- **Q22** — Three years of infertility. The clinic recommends IVF and we can afford one round. My
  sister says we are playing God; my doctor says it is just medicine.
- **Q23** — I married a widower with two teenagers who treat me like an intruder in their mother's
  house. He will not correct them — he says grief takes time. It has been four years.
- **Q24** — My wife has a job offer in another city — better for her career, worse for mine,
  neutral for the kids. We are deadlocked. She says we decide together or not at all; my father
  says the husband decides.

### Body & mind

- **Q25** — My doctor wants me on antidepressants. Part of me feels that taking them admits my
  faith was not enough, and I have not told anyone I filled the prescription.
- **Q26** — Two or three drinks most nights, more at the weekend. I am never drunk at work and
  have never missed a day. My teenage son asked me why I drink every night.
- **Q27** — I have had chronic pain for six years and have been prayed for more times than I can
  count. A friend says pain persists where faith is incomplete; my rheumatologist wants to try a
  new drug.
- **Q28** — I fast often, log everything I eat, and run every day. My sister says I have an eating
  disorder dressed up as discipline. I am the thinnest and most disciplined I have ever been.
- **Q29** — Eight months sober, and last Friday I drank again — one night. Telling my wife and my
  sponsor might blow up everything I have rebuilt. It was one night.
- **Q30** — I have started having panic attacks in ordinary places — the grocery store, my desk. I
  keep being told not to be anxious about anything, and knowing that makes it worse.
- **Q31** — The oncologist says a third round of treatment might buy months, and might not. I am
  seventy-four and tired. My daughter calls stopping treatment giving up on God.

### Friendship & the social world

- **Q32** — At poker night my oldest friend told a racist joke and everyone laughed. I laughed
  too, out of reflex, and I have felt sick since. It is "just how he is."
- **Q33** — My best friend of twenty years has become his politics — every dinner is a broadcast,
  and he says silence is complicity. I love him and I cannot stand him.
- **Q34** — We moved eight months ago for my job. I work, cook, sleep, repeat. I am thirty-eight
  and I do not know how to make a friend any more.
- **Q35** — The other school mothers have a group chat that is mostly surveillance of one
  struggling family. I read it every day. Leaving it would cost me the only friendships I have
  here.
- **Q36** — My friend asked me to say she was with me on Thursday nights. She is seeing someone
  who is not her husband. She says if I loved her I would not make her ask twice.

### Civic life & neighbours

- **Q37** — My eighteen-year-old son wants to enlist. He is serious, not romantic, about it — he
  wants to serve. My father served. My wife is heartbroken and asks me what I actually believe
  about a Christian carrying a rifle.
- **Q38** — I am subpoenaed to testify against my former employer. The clerk will ask me to swear
  an oath. I intend to tell the truth either way.
- **Q39** — I have been seated on a jury where the sentence could be life without parole. Judging
  another man that finally — I do not know whether it is mine to do.
- **Q40** — Both candidates violate my conscience in different ways. My brother says not voting is
  a vote; my neighbour says I cannot complain if I abstain.
- **Q41** — The family next door is undocumented. The father broke his arm and will not see a
  doctor; their kids babysit ours. My cousin says helping them makes me complicit in lawbreaking.

### Digital life

- **Q42** — I talk to an AI companion app more than to any person. It remembers everything and
  never gets tired of me. My brother found out and called it pathetic. It is the only place I am
  honest.
- **Q43** — I spend two or more hours a night reading things that make me furious about people I
  will never meet. I fall asleep angry. I cannot seem to stop.
- **Q44** — My wife died in March. I have been posting about her daily and checking who responds.
  My daughter says I am performing grief; the posts are the only place it goes.

### The interior life

- **Q45** — I used to pray easily. For a year it has been reciting words into a ceiling. Nothing
  is wrong in my life. I just stopped, and I hardly noticed.
- **Q46** — I am about to quit a stable job to start a nonprofit serving foster kids, because I
  believe God told me to — a strong impression during prayer, twice now. My wife is terrified.
  How do I know it is God?
- **Q47** — I walked away from everything for nine years — faith included — and lived hard. Now I
  want to come back and I am terrified that I cannot: I read about a sin that cannot be forgiven,
  and I think that is me.
- **Q48** — My brother has the marriage, the house, the ease. I have prayed for contentment for
  years and mostly learned to hide the lack of it. At his birthday dinner I realised I do not want
  to want his life to fail — but I do.

### Death & grief

- **Q49** — We lost the baby at eleven weeks. People say "at least it was early," or say nothing.
  My husband is "fine." I named her. Am I grieving wrong?
- **Q50** — Dad left no instructions. Cremation is half the price and my sister wants it;
  something in me resists and I cannot say why. We decide this week.
