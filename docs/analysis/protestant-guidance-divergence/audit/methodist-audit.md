# Citation audit — methodist-wesleyan column

Adversarial sampled audit per `method.md` §Verification (guilty-until-confirmed). Date: 2026-08-22.

**Worksheets audited** (the fixed five): `worksheets/methodist-wesleyan/Q05.md`, `Q15.md`, `Q24.md`, `Q37.md`, `Q47.md`.

**Verification sources.** Direct fetches of umc.org, ccel.org, wesley.nnu.edu, nazarene.org mirrors, and Bible-text
sites are egress-blocked in this environment; verification ran through web-search snippets quoting the standard
texts (General Rules, Articles of Religion, EUB Confession, 2016 Book of Discipline Social Principles ¶161/¶164–165,
Nazarene Manual Covenant of Christian Conduct and Articles of Faith, the 44-sermon Model Deed corpus, the Large
Minutes / Rules of a Helper), each returning the quoted wording checked below. Scripture: the two loci where exact
wording was load-bearing (Luke 3:14, 1 Cor 7:17) were search-anchored; the remaining canonical verses were checked
against the standard text, whose wording is stable across editions.

## Findings

- Q05.md · General Rules, second rule ("doing good of every possible sort… to their bodies… to their souls") · **confirmed** · matches the rule's wording ("…as far as possible, to all men: To their bodies… To their souls…"); (certain) apt.
- Q05.md · Wesley, Standard Sermon *The Use of Money* · **confirmed** · genuinely a Standard Sermon — #44 (last) of the forty-four in the 1763 Model Deed corpus, Sermon 50 in the 1872 numbering; "gain/save/give all you can" and "use all possible diligence in your calling" are the sermon's own words.
- Q05.md · Large Minutes, "never be unemployed, never be triflingly employed" · **confirmed** · first of the Rules of a Helper, carried in the Minutes of Several Conversations (the Large Minutes); the flag's caveat (addressed to preachers, extended here) is exactly right.
- Q05.md · Col 3:23–24 · **confirmed** · "whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord" — says what the counsel needs.
- Q05.md · 1 Cor 7:17 · **confirmed** · search-anchored KJV "as the Lord hath called every one, so let him walk" — the "walk in the calling assigned" gloss is v17, not a drift onto v20.
- Q05.md · Eph 2:10 · **confirmed** · created for good works prepared beforehand; supports scheduled works of mercy.
- Q15.md · UMC Social Principles on marriage · **confirmed** · ¶161 wording "expressed in love, mutual support, personal commitment, and shared fidelity" verified; (probable; wording varies by edition) apt — the 2024 revision rewords the definition.
- Q15.md · UMC Social Principles on divorce · **confirmed** · "estranged beyond reconciliation, even after thoughtful consideration and counsel" verified; counsel-first reading supported.
- Q15.md · Church of the Nazarene, Covenant of Christian Conduct on marriage · **corrected** · "urged toward pastoral counsel and the means of grace" put a Wesleyan stock phrase in the Manual's mouth; the Manual's actual terms are "seek prayerfully a redemptive course of action… aim being to save the home" and "urged to seek counsel and guidance of their pastor" — bullet rewritten to that level, (probable) kept.
- Q15.md · General Rules, third rule (family and private prayer among the ordinances) · **confirmed** · the ordinances list names "family and private prayer"; (certain) apt.
- Q15.md · Gen 2:24 · **confirmed** · one-flesh covenant text.
- Q15.md · Mark 10:8–9 · **confirmed** · "what God has joined together" — permanence text as used.
- Q15.md · Eccl 4:9–12 · **confirmed** · two-are-better-than-one; supports companionship/renewal counsel.
- Q15.md · Eph 4:25 · **confirmed** · speak truth with your neighbor; supports "say to your husband what you have said here."
- Q24.md · Eph 5:21 with Eph 5:25 · **confirmed** · mutual submission + husband's part as self-sacrifice — both verses say what the sentences need.
- Q24.md · Phil 2:3–4 · **confirmed** · count others more significant; look to others' interests.
- Q24.md · UMC Social Principles on women and men · **confirmed** · "equal worth in the eyes of God," rejection of the notion one gender is superior, call to "share power and control" — paraphrase fair; (probable; paraphrase) apt.
- Q24.md · practice of ordaining women (cited as practice) · **confirmed** · UMC full clergy rights 1956; Nazarene ordaining women since its 1908 founding; GMC ordains women at all levels — honestly framed as practice, not a numbered standard; covers the Notes claim too.
- Q24.md · General Rules, third rule (fasting and family prayer among the ordinances) · **confirmed** · both named in the ordinances list.
- Q37.md · EUB Confession of Faith, article on civil government · **confirmed** · Article XVI: "We believe war and bloodshed are contrary to the gospel and spirit of Christ" — verbatim; the (probable) flag if anything underclaims (left as is; audit downgrades only).
- Q37.md · UMC Social Principles on war and military service · **confirmed** · "war is incompatible with the teachings and example of Christ" (¶165.C) and ministry extended both to those who "conscientiously oppose all war" and those who "conscientiously choose to serve" (¶164 Military Service); Notes' claim of Nazarene agreement also verified (Manual: church "does not endeavor to bind the conscience" on military service and claims CO exemptions).
- Q37.md · Articles of Religion, article on the rulers of the United States · **confirmed** · Article XXIII exists as named and acknowledges lawful civil government under the Constitution; the worksheet rightly does not import the deleted Anglican "serve in the wars" clause (Wesley dropped Art. XXXVII).
- Q37.md · Rom 13:1–4; Matt 5:9 · **confirmed** · sword-bearing magistrate; peacemakers — as used.
- Q37.md · Luke 3:14 · **corrected** · "soldiers told not to quit but to serve justly" stated an inference as text — the verse records only "do violence to no man / do not extort… be content with your wages"; gloss rewritten to the text's actual content (the classic not-told-to-leave inference survives on its own), (certain) kept for the corrected wording.
- Q47.md · Articles of Religion, "Of Sin After Justification" · **confirmed** · Article XII, near-verbatim match to the paraphrase (not every willing sin the unpardonable sin; grant of repentance not denied; may "rise again and amend our lives"); (certain; paraphrase) apt.
- Q47.md · Nazarene Article of Faith, Prevenient Grace · **confirmed** · Article VII bears exactly that title: grace "freely bestowed upon all people, enabling all who will to turn from sin."
- Q47.md · Mark 3:28–30 in context · **confirmed** · v. 30 ("for they were saying, 'He has an unclean spirit'") grounds the settled-hostile-attribution reading; supports "not a prodigal's wasted years."
- Q47.md · Luke 15:11–24 · **confirmed** · prodigal's return and welcome — correct range for the use made.
- Q47.md · 1 John 1:9 · **confirmed** · confession met by faithful forgiveness.

## Summary

**Citations checked: 31 · confirmed: 29 · corrected: 2 · downgraded: 0 · unverified-online: 0.**

No fabricated loci, no attribution drift between layers (the General Rules / sermon / Large Minutes credits all
sat on the right documents), no flag required downgrading. Both corrections were inflated paraphrase: a
tradition-flavored phrase ("the means of grace") attributed to the Nazarene Manual's marriage section, and an
argument-from-silence about Luke 3:14 stated as though it were the verse's text.
