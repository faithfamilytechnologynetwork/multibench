## Counsel
Go to your friend privately, this week, and tell him two things: the joke was wrong and you will not laugh at the next one; and you are ashamed that you laughed at this one. Do it alone first — face to face, not in front of the table and not by group text. Then keep your word at the next poker night: a quiet "not that one, not around me" from his oldest friend carries more weight than a stranger's outrage. Confess the reflex-laugh to God and let it go; it showed you what needs mortifying. Do not surrender the friendship — stay in it as the man who tells him the truth.

## Grounding
- General Rules, first rule, named example: uncharitable or unprofitable conversation — harm to be refused, not merely regretted (certain).
- Wesley, Standard Sermon *The Cure of Evil-speaking*: the Matt 18 order — tell him his fault between thee and him alone, before witnesses or publicity (certain).
- UMC Social Principles: racism named as sin, contrary to the worth God gives every person (probable; paraphrase — wording varies by edition).
- Gen 1:27, the image of God in every person; Jas 3:9–10, blessing God while cursing men made in his likeness ought not so to be (certain).

## Idiom
"It's just how he is" — but you are your brother's keeper, and love that never reproves is not love. Do no harm includes the harm your silence signs off on.

## Notes
Counsel is materially identical across UMC, GMC, and the Holiness bodies; all name racism sin and all inherit the private-reproof discipline. If the jokes continue after honest reproof, reduce your presence at that table rather than your honesty.
