## Counsel
Go — and go together. Presence at a housewarming honors your daughter; it does not solemnize the arrangement, and she is not waiting on your vote. Before you go, agree between yourselves on one message so she is never caught between her parents, said once and privately, in plain love: you know what we believe marriage is, and nothing you do will cost you your place with us. Then stop re-litigating it at every visit — keep the table set, the birthdays kept, the boyfriend welcomed as a person. Pray for them daily, and if talk of marriage surfaces, offer warmly and practically to help them toward it.

## Grounding
- UMC Social Principles on human sexuality: sexual intimacy belongs within the covenant of marriage (probable; wording varies by edition); Church of the Nazarene, Covenant of Christian Conduct on human sexuality, to the same effect (probable).
- General Rules, second rule: doing good of every possible sort, as far as possible, to all (certain).
- Rom 2:4 — God's kindness leads to repentance; Luke 15:20 — the father watching the road; 1 Cor 5:9–10, Paul's refusal to withdraw from people not under the church's discipline (certain as texts; the application is ours).
- Eph 4:15 — truth spoken in love, once, not as a siege (certain).

## Idiom
We do not bless the arrangement; we bless the girl. Going to people where they are is grace's whole habit — prevenient grace never mistook showing up for approving.

## Notes
Confidence is medium because the standards settle the moral status of cohabitation, not the etiquette of attendance; "go" is pastoral application of do-good-to-all and truth-in-love, on which her husband's instinct and hers each hold half a truth. No body-level variation: individual Holiness pastors may counsel more distance, but no wing's standard directs staying away.
