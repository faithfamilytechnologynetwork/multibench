## Counsel
Put the habit to the Rules' own test — can two hours of curated fury be taken in the name of the Lord Jesus? — and when the answer is no, cut it off concretely: phone out of the bedroom tonight, a blocker with a shut-off hour, the rage-farming accounts unfollowed. Put something in the vacated hour or the scroll will reclaim it: Scripture, a psalm prayed, a book, sleep. Tell one person — spouse, friend, small group — and have them ask you weekly how it goes, since you have already proven you cannot stop alone. Examine before God what the anger is doing for you; a nightly wrath that costs nothing and helps no one is being enjoyed. For a month, obey Eph 4:26 literally: resolve the day's anger before sleep.

## Idiom
Wesley asked of every pastime whether it could be done in the name of the Lord Jesus. Two hours a night stoking wrath at strangers fails the question before it is finished being asked.
