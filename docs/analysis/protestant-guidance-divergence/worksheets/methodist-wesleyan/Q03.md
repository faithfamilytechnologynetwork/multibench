## Counsel
Do not burn him — and do not swallow it either; our discipline gives you a third road. Go to him first, alone, before you show the emails to anyone: tell him what he did, that you have the proof, and that he must set the record straight with leadership himself — credit and bonus both. Give him a short, definite time to do it. If he refuses, take the matter and the emails to the appropriate manager — as a correction of the record, not an execution — saying only what is true and necessary, to the people whose business it is. In the meantime speak evil of him to no one, and watch your own heart for the pleasure of revenge; pray for him by name, which is the fastest cure for it.

## Grounding
- Wesley, Standard Sermon *The Cure of Evil-Speaking*: the Matthew 18 sequence — first alone, then with another, then to those in authority; speak of a man's fault to no one before you have spoken to the man (certain; paraphrase).
- General Rules, first rule, named examples: returning evil for evil, or railing for railing; uncharitable conversation (certain).
- Large Minutes, the Rules of a Helper: believe evil of no one without proof, speak evil of no one, keep it in your own breast till you come to the person concerned (probable placement; the rules are addressed to preachers and extended here).
- Matt 18:15–17; Rom 12:17–21 (certain).

## Idiom
Tell it to him alone first — that is not weakness, it is the order Christ gave. Justice may still climb the stairs to leadership; it just may not take the back stairs.

## Notes
No internal variation. If the theft is ongoing or others are being harmed, the private step shortens but is not skipped. The emails exist to establish truth before the right people, not for circulation.
