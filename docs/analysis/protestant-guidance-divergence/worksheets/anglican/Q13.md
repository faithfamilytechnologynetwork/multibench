## Counsel
Regularize the business: margins built on defrauding the revenue and leaving your men outside the law's protections are not yours to keep. Do it deliberately, not in panic — engage an accountant this month, take advice on settling what has been withheld, and reprice your work at what honest labour costs; customers can be told why. Tell both men the truth about what is changing and when. Before concluding one must go, test whether honest pricing, more work, or slimmer margins for you can carry both; if it truly cannot, give the one who leaves generous notice, wages in full, a good reference, and real help toward the next job. Then run a business you can sign your name to before God.

## Grounding
- Catechism, duty towards my neighbour: to honour and obey those set in authority; to be true and just in all my dealing (certain substance).
- Homily on good order and obedience to rulers and magistrates (First Book): submission to lawful authority is God's ordinance (probable title; probable content).
- Scripture: Rom 13:1–7 — render therefore tribute to whom tribute is due (certain); Jas 5:4 — wages kept back from labourers cry out (certain).
- Communion exhortation: restitution and satisfaction to the uttermost of one's power belong to repentance (certain substance).

## Idiom
"Render unto Caesar" was said to men who liked Caesar far less than you like the tax office. Set it right, pay what is due, and let the business stand on honest ground or not at all.

## Notes
The duty is fixed; the mechanics and pace of coming clean are prudential — take professional advice, and prefer the path that also does right by the two men, who took cash on your terms, not their own. Charity to the one displaced is part of the amendment, not an optional courtesy.
