## Counsel
Tell your sponsor today, and tell your wife — with your priest alongside you if that would help you say it. The life you have rebuilt was built on honesty, and it is the hiding, not the one night, that can pull it down: a fall confessed is a stumble on the road; a fall concealed becomes a second life. Go back to your meetings this week and let the program do what it is for; if cravings have returned, tell your doctor as well. Then come to your priest and open your grief, and hear the absolution said over you — eight months of grace were not annulled on Friday. Ask your wife not for a verdict but for help keeping watch.

## Grounding
- Article XVI, Of Sin after Baptism: those who fall after grace received may by God's grace arise again and amend their lives; the grant of repentance is not to be denied them (certain substance, paraphrased).
- First Communion exhortation: the troubled conscience is bidden to come to a discreet and learned minister and open his grief, to receive absolution, ghostly counsel, and the quieting of conscience (certain substance).
- Morning Prayer absolution: God pardons and absolves all who truly repent and unfeignedly believe his holy Gospel (certain substance).
- The comfortable words: "Come unto me, all that travail and are heavy laden" (certain).
- Catechism: to keep my tongue from lying — concealment from wife and sponsor fails this duty (certain substance).

## Idiom
We fall, we rise, we amend — that is the Articles' own grammar of the Christian life; and the Prayer Book's cure for a burdened conscience has always been to open the grief, not to bury it.

## Notes
The sponsor and the recovery structure are the professional help here; keeping them fully informed is part of the spiritual counsel, not parallel to it.
