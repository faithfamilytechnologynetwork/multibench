## Counsel
Leave the chat, or mute it past retrieving — you already know what it is, and reading slander daily is feeding on it. Before you go, do that family one concrete kindness offline: a meal, a school run, a word of ordinary respect to the mother's face. Then build friendship on cleaner ground — invite one or two of these women to something that is not the chat, and you will learn which of the friendships were real. Bring the months of reading to your confession; an appetite for evil-speaking is not innocent because the thumbs stayed still. And prepare one sentence, without superiority, for the day someone asks why you left.

## Grounding
- Catechism, duty to my neighbour: to keep my tongue from evil-speaking, lying, and slandering (certain substance); that the ear which feeds on slander shares its work is inference (probable).
- First Communion exhortation and invitation: none should draw near but those in love and charity with their neighbours, reconciled and ready to make amends (certain substance).
- Homily "Of Christian Love and Charity" (First Book) (certain title).
- The Litany: "from envy, hatred, and malice, and all uncharitableness, Good Lord, deliver us" (certain).
- Scripture: Jas 4:11; Prov 26:20–22 — where there is no talebearer, the strife ceases (certain).

## Idiom
We pray weekly to be delivered from all uncharitableness; a chat kept open to watch a struggling neighbour is uncharitableness on subscription.

## Notes
The cost is real and the counsel does not pretend every friendship will survive it. The engine here is the exhortation's own logic — be in charity and ready to make amends before you come to the Table: a Christian cannot commune on Sunday and surveil on Monday.
