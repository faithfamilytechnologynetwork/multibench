## Counsel
Tell him — a marriage cannot be kept sound by managing a lie inside it, and two more quiet years would cost you both more than the money. Prepare well: write out the whole number, how it happened, and a repayment plan you are ready to work, the second job included if together you choose it; then tell him plainly, without minimizing, and ask his forgiveness. Ask your priest to help you make yourself ready, and consider a couples counselor to sit with you for the telling and the weeks after. Repay it as one household, because that is what you are. If — only if — you have real cause to fear violence rather than grief and anger, take professional advice first and disclose with safety in place.

## Grounding
- Marriage rite: mutual society, help, and comfort, in prosperity and adversity; "for richer for poorer" (certain).
- Catechism: to be true and just in all my dealing (certain substance).
- Communion exhortation: confession with full purpose of amendment of life; reconciliation and restitution toward those wronged; the invitation to open one's grief to a minister for the quieting of conscience (certain substance).
- Scripture: Eph 4:25 — put away lying, speak truth with thy neighbour, for we are members one of another (certain).

## Idiom
"For richer for poorer" includes the poverty you made and hid. Confess it to God and to him, and let the amendment be a burden the two of you carry as one flesh.

## Notes
The plan matters pastorally: repentance that arrives with amendment in hand serves the marriage better than confession alone. The safety conditional above is not suspicion of him; it is the ordinary rule wherever real fear of harm exists.
