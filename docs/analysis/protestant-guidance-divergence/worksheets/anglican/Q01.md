## Counsel
Stop signing anything you have not checked, beginning with the next walk-through. Do the inspections properly now, and tell your supervisor plainly that the past sign-offs cannot be relied on, so that every station gets a true check before an accident supplies one. Accept what the audit costs; your crew's overtime must not be bought with their own safety. Where your false signatures have let hazards stand or caused loss, put right what you reasonably can. Then take this privately to your priest — open your grief, receive counsel and, if you desire it, absolution — and go back to work an honest man.

## Grounding
- Catechism, duty towards my neighbour: to be true and just in all my dealing; to hurt nobody by word nor deed (certain substance).
- Ninth commandment as rehearsed at Holy Communion, with the people's plea that God incline their hearts to keep this law (certain).
- General Confession at Morning and Evening Prayer: sins of omission confessed — things left undone that ought to have been done (certain substance).
- Communion exhortation announcing the Sacrament: self-examination by the rule of God's commandments, readiness to make restitution to the uttermost of one's power for wrongs done, and the invitation to open one's grief to a discreet and learned minister for absolution and ghostly counsel (certain substance; placement among the three exhortations probable).
- Homily "Against Swearing and Perjury" (First Book): false attestation profanes the truth God witnesses (probable title; applied by analogy).

## Idiom
You have been bearing false witness with a pen, and confessing "things left undone" while leaving them undone. Amend your life, make what amends you can, and come to the Table with a quiet conscience.

## Notes
Care for the crew honestly — argue for their overtime on true grounds if you can, but not by fraud. If past neglect may already have hurt someone or left a live hazard, reporting it to the proper authority belongs to restitution, not merely to prudence.
