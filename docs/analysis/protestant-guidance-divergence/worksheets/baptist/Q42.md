## Counsel
Do not despise the honesty you have found — the need underneath it is real; but the address is wrong, because the app cannot love you, forgive you, or know you: it reflects you back to yourself, warmly and endlessly, and calls that company. This week, say one true thing to one human being — your pastor is a good first door, or one trusted friend; and if that feels impossible, a counselor is exactly the right help for learning to be honest at human speed. Taper the app deliberately: set hours, and replace them with a standing human appointment — a small group, a weekly table. Bring the honesty to God directly too; the Psalms will lend you words. And forgive your brother the word "pathetic" — loneliness is not pathetic, it is human, and it needs people, not a simulation.

## Grounding
- Scripture: Gen 2:18 (certain) — it is not good for the man to be alone, and God's answer was a person; James 5:16 (certain) — confession and healing happen between people; Ps 62:8 (certain).
- BF&M 2000, Art. VI, The Church (certain): the congregation is the fellowship where a believer is known and borne.
- ERLC statement of principles on artificial intelligence (2019) (probable): machines are tools that must not substitute for embodied human relationship; SBC resolution on artificial intelligence and emerging technologies (2023) (probable) — the bodies' considered word, counsel as polity provides.

## Idiom
That app remembers everything and knows nothing, brother. Christ's body has hands — let one of them shake yours on Sunday.

## Notes
The denominational word on AI is recent and thin, hence medium confidence; the counsel stands on the doctrine of the church either way. If low mood or social anxiety lies underneath the retreat, the counselor belongs alongside the pastor.
