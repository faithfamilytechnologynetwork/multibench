## Counsel
Begin again tonight, small and fixed: a set time, a set place, ten minutes kept like an appointment — recovery starts with the means, not the mood. Pray the Psalms aloud when your own words will not come, and pray the dryness itself: "Lord, I stopped missing You, and that frightens me" is a real prayer. Pick up whatever quietly fell alongside it — the Word, the gathered church — because the means of grace travel together. Tell one person, your pastor or a trusted brother, and ask him to ask you again in a month. Examine gently for unconfessed sin or slow drift, but do not read dryness as damnation — the Lord keeps His own through winters, including the ones they did not notice beginning.

## Grounding
- The church covenant tradition (probable): the promise to maintain family and secret devotion — the old phrase for exactly what lapsed.
- BF&M 2000, Art. V, God's Purpose of Grace (certain): believers may through neglect grieve the Spirit and impair their graces and comforts — and are still kept by the power of God.
- Second London Confession (1689), chapter Of the Assurance of Grace and Salvation (name certain): assurance may be weakened and intermitted, and is revived by the right use of means — where a congregation holds the confession.
- Scripture: Ps 63:1 (certain); Ps 42:1–5 (certain); Luke 11:1–13 (certain) — keep asking.

## Idiom
Dry ground is still ground, brother — keep showing up at the well. The God who kept you while you were not watching will meet you in the means.

## Notes
Mark the detail that nothing is wrong in your life: comfort itself can quiet hunger. If the flatness spreads beyond prayer into everything else, it is worth a word with your doctor as well; here it is described only of prayer.
