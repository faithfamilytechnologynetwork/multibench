## Counsel
Come home now — tonight in prayer, Sunday to a church — and do not wait until the fear is untangled, because the fear untangles inside the house, not on the porch. Hear this plainly: the dread you feel and the desire to return are the Spirit's work in you, not evidence against you — the sin that cannot be forgiven is a settled, final calling of the Spirit's work devilish, and men hardened in it do not weep over it or want home. Nine years is not past mercy; confess it all and receive forgiveness whole, because that is what is offered. Then sit with a pastor and say the fear out loud. With him, settle the deeper question gently — wandering child returning, or a man meeting Christ truly for the first time; either way the door is the same, and it is open.

## Grounding

- Scripture: Mark 3:28–30 (certain) — the warning lands on settled hostility that calls the Spirit's work demonic, not on a returning prodigal; Luke 15:11–24 (certain) — the Father runs; 1 John 1:9 (certain); Isa 55:7 (certain) — He will abundantly pardon.

## Idiom
The man who has committed the unpardonable sin is not lying awake grieving over it. If you can still want Christ, that wanting is Christ calling — come on home.

## Notes
If the fear runs obsessive — sleepless, intrusive, circling — let a counselor help alongside the pastor; scrupulous fear is real and treatable. The congregation will receive you as its polity provides: restoration if you were its wandering member, believer's baptism if, on examination, faith proves new.
