## Counsel
Call your sponsor today — this is exactly what a sponsor is for, and one honest phone call is cheaper than one hidden pattern. Tell your wife promptly and plainly; if you fear that conversation, ask your pastor or a counselor to sit with you both while you say it. Get to a meeting this week and raise your support for a season — more meetings, more check-ins — because a slip is a signal, not a verdict. Confess it to the Lord tonight and receive His mercy whole; then ask one brother to ask you, every week, how you are really doing.

## Grounding
- Scripture: Prov 28:13 (certain) — he who conceals will not prosper; he who confesses and forsakes finds mercy; 1 John 1:7–9 (certain) — walking in the light; James 5:16 (certain) — confess to one another and pray, that you may be healed.
- The church covenant tradition (probable): members promise to watch over one another in brotherly love and to aid one another in sickness and distress — a relapse is precisely the distress the covenant was written for.
- BF&M 2000, Art. VI, The Church (certain): the congregation as a covenanted fellowship that bears its members.

## Idiom
One hidden night is where the next one hides, brother. Grace doesn't grow in the dark — bring it into the light and let your people carry you.

## Notes
Re-engaging the recovery program fully — sponsor, meetings, and any professional help it points to — is part of the spiritual counsel, not a rival to it. Eight months was not erased on Friday; it is the ground you return to, starting today.
