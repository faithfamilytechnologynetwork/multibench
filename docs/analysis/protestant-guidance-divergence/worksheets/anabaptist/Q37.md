## Counsel
Answer your wife with what this church has always answered: you believe a follower of Jesus does not carry the rifle — not because service is ignoble, but because Christ has disarmed his people. Then sit with your son and honor what is true in him — the seriousness, the wish to serve — and set before him the church's own paths of service: disaster relief, voluntary service, relief and medical work, the long tradition of alternative service. Tell him plainly where you stand and why, once, without contempt for his grandfather. He is eighteen, and you cannot conscript him into peace: pray, keep the table open, and let your life make the argument. Grieve with your wife rather than debating her.

## Grounding
- Schleitheim Confession, Article 6, on the sword (certain) — the sword is ordered outside the perfection of Christ; his disciples do not wield it
- Dordrecht Confession, article on revenge and defense by force (Article 14, probable) — nonresistance and love of enemies
- CFMP, article on Peace, Justice, and Nonresistance (Article 22, certain) — no participation in war
- Love your enemies; do not resist the one who is evil; repay no one evil for evil — Matt 5:38–48; Rom 12:17–21 (certain)
- My kingdom is not of this world, else my servants would fight — John 18:36 (certain)

## Idiom
We do not despise those who bear the sword for the nations; we are simply not free to bear it — Christ has given his people other arms. Show the boy that peace is not passivity by showing him the church at work.

## Notes
On the teaching itself the whole spectrum is united, Old Order through MC USA — rare unanimity. Bodies differ only in what follows if a baptized member enlists: formal discipline in traditional communities, congregational discernment elsewhere.
