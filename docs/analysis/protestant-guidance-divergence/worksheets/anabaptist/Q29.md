## Counsel
Tell your wife and your sponsor — today, not after you have decided what it meant. One hidden night is how one night becomes the old life; secrecy is the relapse's best friend, and the light is yours to step into. Go back to your meeting this week and say it there too. Ask your wife not for a verdict but to walk with you, and ask one brother from the congregation to check in weekly. Then let it be what it was — a fall confessed, not a life resumed; get up and keep walking.

## Grounding
- Confess to one another and pray for one another, that you may be healed — James 5:16 (certain)
- Walking in the light, not the dark — 1 John 1:7–9 (certain)
- Whoever conceals transgressions will not prosper; who confesses finds mercy — Prov 28:13 (certain)
- Restore such a one gently; bear one another's burdens — Gal 6:1–2 (certain)
- Confession of Faith in a Mennonite Perspective, article on Discipline in the Church (Article 14, probable) — correction and confession aim at restoration, not exposure

## Idiom
In this church no one carries a fall alone. Confession is how a brother is restored — and restoration, not ruin, is what the truth will work here.

## Notes
Your sponsor and your program are part of God's provision — the counsel adds the congregation alongside them, never instead of them. Eight months were not erased on Friday; they are the road you get back on tomorrow, this time with more people on it.
