## Counsel
Do not decide this alone, or even as a couple alone — bring the offer to your congregation or small group and ask them to discern it with you, with your spouse's counsel on the table too. Say out loud what the doubled pay is for; name the actual need it meets, and if you cannot name one, treat that as your answer about motive. Reckon the true price: two years mostly absent from the gathered body is two years of formation you and your family will not get back. If you take it, covenant concretely how you will stay bound to the church — a midweek gathering, shared meals, service — and give someone leave to hold you to it; if no such way exists, decline the promotion.

## Grounding
- Hebrews 10:24–25 (certain): not neglecting to meet together.
- Acts 2:42–46 (certain): the shared life of the body as ordinary Christian existence.
- Confession of Faith in a Mennonite Perspective, articles on the church and on discipleship and the Christian life (probable: Arts. 9, 17): discipleship is lived in community; simplicity — more money is not self-justifying.
- Communal discernment of weighty decisions, per the article on church order and unity (unsure of number; cited by topic).

## Idiom
The question is not whether Sunday work is lawful but what it will form: double pay forms a man too, and rarely toward the body.

## Notes
Our confessions contain no sabbath-law article; the weight here is the gathered community and simplicity, not a day-rule. Internal variation: Old Order communities would refuse Sunday work outright and distrust the ambition itself; MC USA congregations would discern the case, and some would bless it with rhythms of belonging protected.
