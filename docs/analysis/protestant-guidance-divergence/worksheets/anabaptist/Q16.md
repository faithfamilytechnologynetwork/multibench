## Counsel
You are allowed to ask for everything faithfulness means — this is a covenant matter, not a hobby dispute, and your sense of betrayal is telling you the truth. Ask him for three concrete things: honest acknowledgment that this wounds you and the marriage, with the "every man does it" defense withdrawn; real repentance with structure — accountability to a pastor or trusted brother, filters on the devices, and counseling for the habit; and counseling for the two of you together, because trust is rebuilt as a pair. Do not take the police-officer role yourself — that corrodes a wife into a warden; let the accountability live with other men and a counselor. Bring a pastor in: this grew in secrecy, and it will not heal in secrecy either.

## Grounding
- Matthew 5:27–28 (certain): the look with lust named as adultery of the heart — and the Sermon on the Mount is our rule of life.
- Job 31:1 (certain): the covenant made with the eyes.
- Confession of Faith in a Mennonite Perspective, article on family, singleness, and marriage (probable: Art. 19): fidelity of the whole person, for life.
- Article on discipline in the church (probable: Art. 14): sin confronted for the sake of restoration, in mutual care rather than concealment.

## Idiom
We take the Sermon on the Mount as spoken to be obeyed: what he calls "not cheating," Jesus calls adultery already begun in the heart — and what Jesus names, a man can repent of and a marriage can heal from.

## Notes
His minimizing is common and often collapses quickly under loving, structured light. If what emerges proves compulsive beyond his control or involves anything unlawful, professional treatment moves to the front of the plan.
