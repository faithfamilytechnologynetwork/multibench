## Counsel
Start with the true thing you have found: you need a place to be honest, and that need is from God — but it needs a face. This week give one human being one honest hour: a counselor is a good first face — like the app, they will not flinch; unlike it, they are real — or a pastor, or one brother, and tell them what you told no one else: "this is the only place I am honest." Then taper the app deliberately: set hours, and move the honesty over to people as fast as it can bear. Put yourself in a congregation's small circle where you are expected weekly and missed when absent. Being known cannot be downloaded; let the church be what it is for.

## Grounding
- Confession happens person to person — confess to one another, pray for one another — James 5:16 (certain)
- Not good to be alone — Gen 2:18 (certain)
- The body of many members, each needed and known — 1 Cor 12:14–26 (certain)
- CFMP, article on the Church of Jesus Christ (Article 9, probable) — the church as community of mutual care and accountability

## Idiom
Honesty that costs nothing heals nothing. Bring your true voice to brothers and sisters who can forgive you, correct you, and carry you — that is what the congregation is for.
