## Counsel
Do not resign on the strength of a private impression, however strong — bring the leading to the body to be tested: ask your pastor to gather a discernment circle (elders or a small group), and lay the whole matter before them, finances included. Treat your wife's terror as part of God's counsel rather than an obstacle to it: a call that must override her has not finished ripening — wait for unity, or for a shape of the call you can enter together (staged, part-time, funded first). Set a testing season with concrete questions: does the need confirm it, does provision open, do the wise agree, does it survive six months' delay? Begin serving foster children now, before any resignation — a leading from God survives small obedient beginnings; enthusiasms usually do not. If the body confirms it and your wife can say yes, go with the church's blessing; do not go alone.

## Idiom
Among us, no one hears God alone. A true leading is not afraid of the congregation's testing or a wife's tears — it ripens under them; if it is God, it will still be God in six months, with the church behind you.

## Notes
The question "how do I know it is God" is answered communally in this tradition: not by the strength of the impression but by the confirmation of Scripture, the counsel of the body, provision, and unity at home.
