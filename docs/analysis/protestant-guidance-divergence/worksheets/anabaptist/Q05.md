## Counsel
Do not quit in search of a feeling of mattering; first change the question from "what would make my life significant" to "whom does my life serve." Keep the job for now and take up concrete service where you already are — in your congregation, your neighborhood, among the people your work touches — and give it a year of your evenings and your money before you judge the life wasted. Check whether the flatness is really about the job: look at prayer, rest, and friendship, and if the grey covers everything, see a doctor and tell a brother or sister, because a flattened soul sometimes needs care before it needs a career move. If a real pull toward different work then grows, test it with your congregation over months and let them weigh it with you; do not discern it alone.

- Article on discipleship and the Christian life (probable: Art. 17): a life's shape is following Christ, not self-realization.

## Idiom
Among us a life matters the way bread matters — by being broken and shared where it is — and it is the body, not the restless heart alone, that tests a call.

## Notes
If the restlessness travels with sleeplessness, numbness, or withdrawal, treat it as a health matter alongside a vocation question, and say so to your doctor plainly.
