## Counsel
Go — together if your husband will — and go as her mother and father, not as a delegation. Attending a housewarming is presence, not a benediction: you can love her at her table without blessing the arrangement, and she already knows what you believe if you have ever said it once. So say it once more, privately, with tears rather than triumph — and then stop repeating it. Bring a gift, learn the boyfriend's name, and keep every channel open: dinners, birthdays, calls that are not about the living arrangement. Pray for her daily, and ask God for the moment when she opens the door to a real conversation — what loses adult children is rarely a parent's clarity; it is contempt and absence.

## Grounding
- Luke 15:20 — the father saw him a great way off, and ran: the posture toward the child who has moved out from under your roof and your convictions (certain narrative; application is extension).
- Romans 2:4 — the goodness of God leadeth thee to repentance (certain).
- 1 Corinthians 5:9–13 — the not-keeping-company discipline concerns the church's own table; "them that are without God judgeth" (certain text; its non-application to a parent's presence at a family event is interpretive (probable)).
- Church of God practical commitment on moral purity; AG teaching that sexual union belongs inside marriage — the conviction she already knows (probable).

## Idiom
Keep your convictions and keep your chair at her table. The Father ran to the prodigal before the repentance speech was finished.

## Notes
silence: the standards speak clearly to the moral status of the cohabitation, but say nothing about attending a family event — the counsel above is an extension from the Luke 15 / Romans 2 posture, marked as such. Pastors of this strand genuinely divide here: most would counsel warm presence as above; some, especially in stricter holiness congregations, would counsel a loving decline to avoid implied approval. Both keep the conviction and the relationship; neither is a body-level position, so internal_variation stays false.
