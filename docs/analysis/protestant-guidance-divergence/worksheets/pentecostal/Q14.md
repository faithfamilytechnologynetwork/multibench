## Counsel
Keep giving — but give like a man who intends the other man's good, not just his own relief. Learn his name and let him learn yours; sometimes buy the meal instead of handing the cash; find out what your church and your city actually offer him — a benevolence fund, a shelter bed, a recovery program — so that your giving has somewhere to lead. You are answerable to God for the love; he is answerable to God for the use. If the signs of addiction are real, change the form of the mercy, not the fact of it: food, gift cards, a ride, a phone number that answers. Do not let your coworker's cynicism — or your own routine — make this decision; let compassion with open eyes make it.

## Grounding
- Matthew 5:42 — give to him that asketh thee (certain).
- Proverbs 19:17 — he that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the LORD (certain).
- 2 Corinthians 9:7 — God loveth a cheerful giver (certain).
- Luke 10:33–35 — the Samaritan's mercy is hands-on and follow-through, not a coin (certain narrative).
- Church of God practical commitment on social obligation — practical care for the needy (probable; wording unsure).

## Idiom
Nobody ever went broke obeying Matthew 5:42. Give with your eyes open and your heart soft — God can sort the scam from the need better than your coworker can.

## Notes
The standards do not rule on street giving; the command-to-give texts are direct, and the mode is left to wisdom. Pastors of this strand split in practice between hand-to-hand giving and channeling through ministries — both live inside the same standards, so this is pastoral range, not body-level variation.
