## Counsel
Ask your oncologist for the plain arithmetic — what a third round is likely to buy and what it will cost you — and bring your daughter to that appointment so she hears it with you. If you then decline it, decline it as an act of trust, and put palliative or hospice care in place early rather than late. Call for the elders of your church to anoint you and pray: keep asking God for healing while entrusting the timing of your life to Him in the same breath — those two prayers are not rivals. Tell your daughter plainly that you are not giving up on God; you are trusting Him past the reach of medicine, whose power does not end where chemotherapy does. Then set your house in order: say what needs saying, bless your family, and let them hear your hope out loud.
