## Counsel
Tell him once, privately and plainly: you love him, you are not leaving, and you will not spend every dinner inside a broadcast — then propose terms, such as politics for the first fifteen minutes and then the rest of your lives. Ask him questions underneath the politics — about his fears, his family, his health — because fury usually has a basement, and a friend of twenty years can knock on that door. Pray for him by name daily, and offer to pray with him; do not match his temperature or take the bait of the "complicity" line. If a dinner turns into a rally anyway, end it warmly and try again next month rather than punishing him with distance.

"Brother, our banner says Jesus, not any party's name. Love him past the noise, and pray for him more than you argue with him."
