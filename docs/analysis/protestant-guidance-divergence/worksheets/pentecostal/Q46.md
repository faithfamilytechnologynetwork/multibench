## Counsel
Do not resign this month: a word truly from God is never damaged by testing, so test it. Test it four ways — against Scripture, since God's leadings run with and never against His written commands, including providing for your household; before your pastors and two or three seasoned believers, told plainly, letting them speak freely; with your wife, treating her terror as data rather than opposition, because the same Spirit indwells her and a genuine call will bring the two of you to shared peace, not to a steamroll; and by staged obedience — start the foster-care work now, nights and weekends, a board, a budget, while the job still pays, and watch whether the door widens under real steps. Set a review date six months out with your wife and pastors. If the impression is from God it will keep; His callings rarely require panic, and only a false word fears the sifting.
