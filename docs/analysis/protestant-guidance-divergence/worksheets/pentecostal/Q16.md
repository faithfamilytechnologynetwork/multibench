## Counsel
You are allowed to call this what it is — unfaithfulness of the eyes and heart — and you are allowed to ask for real repentance, not a debate about what "every man" does. Ask him for three concrete things: full honesty about the extent of it; accountability that is not you — software on the devices and a man he answers to, pastor or trusted brother; and counseling, together, and individually for him with someone experienced in sexual integrity. Refuse the police-officer role yourself; it will crush you, and his accountability must run to other men. Get care for your own wound — a pastor's wife, a counselor, one safe friend — betrayal grief is real grief, not to be carried alone. Rebuild intimacy on the far side of repentance, not as a substitute for it; if he refuses all of it, bring the marriage to your pastor rather than absorbing it in silence.
