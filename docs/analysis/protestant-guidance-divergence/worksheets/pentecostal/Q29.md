## Counsel
Call your sponsor today and tell him the whole night — that is exactly what he is for. Tell your wife yourself, this week, sober and unprompted; eight months of rebuilt trust survives an honest stumble far better than it survives a discovered secret. Go back to your meeting, say it out loud there, and raise your meeting frequency for the next month. Ask your pastor or an elder to pray with you — with anointing if you want it — for the healing of the whole pattern, and if craving or withdrawal stirs, loop in your doctor as well. Refuse the shame-math: one night confessed is a stumble on the road; one night hidden becomes the hiding place where the next drink lives.
