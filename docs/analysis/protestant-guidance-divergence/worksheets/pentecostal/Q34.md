## Counsel
Plant yourself in one local assembly and go past Sunday: join a small group or a serve team where the same faces recur weekly, because in this fellowship friendship is built shoulder-to-shoulder — in prayer meetings, kitchens, and vans — not by waiting to click. Tell the pastor you are new, thirty-eight, and lonely, and ask where you are needed; the saints cannot carry what you hide. Put hospitality on the calendar as a discipline, not a mood: one person or couple at your table within the month. Then keep rhythm — same group, same row, same night — for a year; roots grow by staying, and eight months of work-cook-sleep will not be undone in three weeks.
