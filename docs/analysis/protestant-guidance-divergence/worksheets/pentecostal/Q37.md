## Counsel
Tell your wife and son honestly where this fellowship stands: bearing arms in the nation's service is left to each believer's conscience — the movement honors the serious soldier and equally protects the conscientious objector, so your son may enlist without sin and could also decline without shame. That means the work now is discernment, not verdict: have him write out his reasons, and set a season of prayer — and fasting, as this house knows how — before anything is signed. Put him in front of a believing veteran and, if you can, a chaplain, so he hears the cost from people who have carried it. Tell your wife her heartbreak is not faithlessness and will not be steamrolled; nothing this weighty is decided in your family without praying through it together. If his conscience is clear and his purpose steady, send him with your blessing rather than your silence.
