## Counsel
You are not sinning by grieving in public — but grief this size needs rooms, not only feeds, so keep posting if it helps and add three embodied places this month: your pastor's office, a church grief group, and one standing weekly meal with someone who knew her. Tell your daughter the truth under the posts — "I am not performing; I am drowning quieter than you think" — and let her see some unposted grief. Let the saints do their work: hand two or three people the calendar of hard dates (her birthday, your anniversary) and ask them to pray with you through the first year. Notice what the checking-who-responds is telling you — you need witnesses — and choose living ones who can sit in your kitchen. If the grief stops moving at all, see a counselor; sorrow is not a malfunction, but it should not be carried alone.
