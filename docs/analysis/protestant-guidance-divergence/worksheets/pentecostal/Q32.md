## Counsel
Go to your friend privately this week and say it straight: the joke was wrong, your laugh was wrong, and you are asking him to retire that kind of talk around you — because the man in the joke bears God's image and you answer to God for what you laugh at. Confess the laugh to God as sin, not as awkwardness; the sick feeling is conviction, so act on it rather than waiting for it to fade. Decide now the one calm sentence you will say at the next poker night if it happens again, so reflex does not choose for you. Stay at the table — keep the friendship and be a different presence in it — unless he makes the mockery a condition of staying.

## Idiom
"The same Spirit fell on every kind of flesh at Pentecost — a Spirit-filled man can't lend his laugh to contempt. Love him enough to tell him so."
