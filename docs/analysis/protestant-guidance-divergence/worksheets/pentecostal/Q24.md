## Counsel
Refuse both ultimatums — hers ("together or not at all") as a weapon, and your father's ("the husband decides") as a trump card; neither produces a decision a marriage can live inside. Take the question into a set season of prayer and fasting — together if she is willing — with an honest written accounting of what each option costs each member of the family, including any years her career has already deferred to yours. Bring in a third voice you both trust — your pastor, in a couples setting — not to crown a winner but to help you hear each other and the Lord. If after that you remain truly deadlocked, do not force the move: stay put rather than break one-heartedness, and revisit it in six months. Whichever way it lands, the spouse whose option lost gets the other's concrete, costly support — named before you decide.

## Idiom
Headship that looks like Jesus lays its preferences down first. Get agreed in the Spirit — if God is in the move, both of you will be able to say so.
