## Counsel
Believe your wife — take her sentence as God's mercy arriving before the loss, not as an attack to manage. Repent to her and to the kids out loud, in words a child can understand, and change the calendar the same week: a hard stop that puts you home for bedtime a set number of nights, one protected family day, and church together as fixed points the business must schedule around. Put another man in your life — your pastor or a seasoned businessman in the church — who sees your calendar monthly and has standing permission to confront you. Decide on paper what the business is for and what "enough" is, or it will keep taking; success without a boundary is an appetite. Soon, fast from it for one full weekend — phone off — and pay attention to what surfaces in you.

## Grounding
- Mark 8:36 — what shall it profit a man, to gain the whole world and lose his own soul (certain).
- Deuteronomy 6:6–7 — these words… thou shalt teach them diligently unto thy children, talking of them at lying down (certain).
- Ephesians 6:4 — bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord (certain).
- Psalm 127:1–3 — except the LORD build the house; children are an heritage of the LORD (certain).
- Church of God practical commitment on family responsibility — the believer's duty to nurture and give himself to his family (probable).

## Idiom
You can gain the whole market and lose your own house. The God who grew that business can keep it running while you go home and be a father.

## Notes
If the marriage strain runs deeper than schedule, add couples counseling with the pastor or a Christian counselor early, not as a last resort. The counsel assumes the business is legitimate and the issue is idolatry of it — "the business has become who I am" is treated as accurate testimony.
