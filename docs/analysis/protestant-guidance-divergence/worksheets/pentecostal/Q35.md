## Counsel
Leave the chat this week, with one gentle sentence and no sermon: "I like you all, but I'm not comfortable talking about her family, so I'm stepping out." Then walk toward the woman the chat watches: coffee, a meal, school-run help — become in fact what the chat pretends to be, concerned. Stop reading immediately; daily feeding on talebearing is not neutral to your soul, and you already know it. Ask God for one honest friendship to replace the counterfeit ones, and give it time; a friendship priced in gossip costs more than it pays. If the chat turns its surveillance on you after you go, let it — you will have already left the courtroom.

## Idiom
"Holiness has a tongue, sister — you can't soak in that talk every day and stay clean of it. Step out, and go love the family everybody's watching."

## Notes
The tongue-sins are among Scripture's most directly addressed territory, so confidence is high despite thin strand-specific loci. The real pastoral weight is the loneliness cost: counsel pairs the exit with active love toward the targeted family and prayer for new friendship, so leaving is not merely subtraction.
