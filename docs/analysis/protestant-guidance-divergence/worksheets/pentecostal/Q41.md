## Counsel
Help them, starting with the arm: a broken arm needs setting, so find the community health clinic or urgent care that treats regardless of status, offer to drive him, sit with him, and help with the bill if you can. Keep your table, your kids, and their babysitting arrangement open — mercy to the family actually in front of you is commanded neighbor-love, not a legal strategy. Answer your cousin from Scripture rather than from politics: binding a wound is not lawbreaking, and the law of Christ has always reached the stranger; you are not forging papers, you are feeding neighbors. Ask your church quietly about benevolence help, and offer to pray with the family if they welcome it — as guests in their trust, not as leverage.
