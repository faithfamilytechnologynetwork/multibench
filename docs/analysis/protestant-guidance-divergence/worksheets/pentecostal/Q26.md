## Counsel
Stop drinking — not cut back, stop — and pour out what is in the house this week rather than finishing it. Answer your son straight: tell him he asked a better question than he knows, that you do not have an answer you are proud of, and that you are quitting; then let him watch you keep your word. Tell your wife and one trusted brother the same decision so it has witnesses who can ask you about it next month. And take your own report seriously — every night, more at weekends: if stopping proves harder than you expect, say so without shame to your doctor or a recovery group; that is wisdom, not weakness.
