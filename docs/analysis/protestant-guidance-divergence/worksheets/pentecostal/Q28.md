## Counsel
Take your sister seriously enough to test her word: book a medical evaluation this month — your doctor, and a counselor who knows eating disorders — and tell her the appointment date. Lay the fasting down until someone responsible for your health says otherwise; a fast you cannot stop is no longer a fast unto God. Stop the food logging for a season and let someone else set your running load. Tell your pastor what your sister said and ask him to pray with you and to ask you about it again in a month. Measure discipline by freedom: if the practice cannot rest, it has become the master.

## Grounding
- Matthew 6:16–18 — fasting directed to the Father, not to the mirror or the record (certain)
- Isaiah 58 — the fast God chooses is measured by mercy, not by self-mastery (certain)
- 1 Corinthians 6:19–20 — the body as the Spirit's temple, to be stewarded not punished (certain)
- Statement of Fundamental Truths, Divine Healing article (certain), with the movement's settled posture that seeking medical care is not unbelief (probable)
- Church of God (Cleveland) Practical Commitments, spiritual example — fasting among commended disciplines (unsure)

## Idiom
"Fasting is unto the Lord, sister — the day it starts being unto the scale, lay it down and let Him feed you."
