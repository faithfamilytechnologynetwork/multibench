## Counsel
You are genuinely free before God here: vote for the candidate your conscience can bear, vote a third option or write in, or in an extremity abstain — none of these is sin, and no one may bind your conscience where God has not. Do the work before you use the freedom: pray over it, read past the noise, and weigh which outcome you believe will do your neighbors the most good, then decide in faith — what is not of faith is sin. Refuse both your brother's formula and your neighbor's; they are pressure, not Scripture. Once you have decided, pray for whoever wins — that command is not optional — and do not let an election cost you a brother or a neighbor.
