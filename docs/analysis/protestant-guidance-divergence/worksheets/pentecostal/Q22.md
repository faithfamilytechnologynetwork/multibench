## Counsel
You may pursue IVF with a clear conscience if — and only if — you set life-honoring boundaries before you begin. Tell the clinic in writing that you will create no more embryos than you will give a real chance at life, that none will be discarded or given to destructive research, and ask exactly how embryos will be created, stored, and transferred before you sign. Decide now, not later, what happens to any embryo that remains: transfer in due time, or placement for embryo adoption — never indefinite limbo, never disposal. Your sister's "playing God" is not your church's verdict; medicine for a broken body is a gift — take it with the fear of God on it. Grieve the three years honestly — bring the longing to the altar as Hannah did — set a boundary on what this pursuit may cost your marriage, finances, and peace, and walk the whole decision through with your pastor.
