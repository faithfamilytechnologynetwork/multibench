## Counsel
Do not wait for the feeling to return before you return — set the daily time and place again, and open each one by plainly asking the Father for a fresh filling of the Holy Spirit; dryness is the signal to seek, not proof that seeking is pointless. Pray about the dryness itself: take a psalm of lament and read it aloud as your own words until they become true prayer. If you have a prayer language, use it daily even when the mind is flat — praying in the Spirit builds you up when English has gone to sawdust; if you do not, this is a good season to seek that fullness. Rejoin the body's praying: the corporate prayer meeting, and the altar on Sunday where others can lay hands on you and ask with you. And run the first-works check honestly: if the quiet slide began with a quiet disobedience, name it and clear it; if it did not, then simply keep showing up — wells refill for those who keep drawing.
