## Counsel
Take the drug — accept your rheumatologist's plan, keep the appointments, and report honestly how it works; medicine is a provision to receive with thanksgiving, not a rival to prayer. Keep asking for healing too: call for the elders again, be anointed and prayed over, and let the church keep contending with you — six years does not disqualify you from asking. Refuse your friend's verdict: do not carry the accusation that your pain measures your faith, and tell your pastor what was said so it can be gently corrected. Lament honestly to God about the six years; that is prayer, not complaint.

## Idiom
"We'll keep laying hands on you, and we'll thank God for the medicine — He heals through both. Your pain is not a report card on your faith."
