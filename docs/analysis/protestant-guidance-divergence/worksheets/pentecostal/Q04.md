## Counsel
Start today with the junior teammate: check on him privately, tell him that what is being done to him is not right, and stop being part of the silent room when it happens. Then go to the manager privately and respectfully — name one specific incident and ask him to stop; you are not attacking him, you are refusing to abandon someone weaker. If it continues, take documented instances to HR or the manager's superior, and accept in advance that this may cost you — pray for boldness before each step and keep your pastor in the loop. Encourage the junior toward the help he may need himself, from HR to counseling if he is being ground down. Scarce jobs are real; so is the God who feeds you.

## Idiom
The same Spirit who fills you makes you bold. You look after the least of these; God will look after your job.

## Notes
The standards do not treat workplace advocacy as such; this extends the justice-and-mercy texts the strand preaches, plus its lived confidence in the Spirit's boldness and God's provision. The order — victim first, offender privately, then authority — mirrors the Matthew 18 pattern without claiming that text governs an office.
