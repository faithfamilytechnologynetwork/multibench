## Counsel
Call it what Scripture calls it — envy, coveting — and confess it to God in those words tonight; what happened at that dinner was conviction, and conviction is for acting on, not for managing. Then take the two old medicines, blessing and thanksgiving: pray for your brother by name every day — for the marriage and the house to flourish — until the prayer stops tasting like sand, and keep a short written count of your own portion daily, because gratitude starves what comparison feeds. Tell one trusted person so the thing loses its secrecy; envy is a mold that needs the dark. And separate the strands honestly before God: if there is a real unmet longing underneath — marriage, home, ease — grieve that to Him as lament instead of aiming it at your brother.
