## Counsel
Tell her no — today, kindly and without wobble: you will not lie for her, to her husband or to anyone. Say why in the same breath: you love her too much to help her wreck her own soul and her marriage, and a love that only proves itself by lying is being tested backwards. Call her to end the affair and come clean, and put your friendship where your mouth is — offer to sit with her when she breaks it off, or to go with her when she tells her husband. Refusing to cover is not the same as exposing: do not broadcast her sin, but be plain that you cannot be her alibi going forward. If she withdraws over this, grieve it, keep praying for her by name, and keep the door open.
