## Counsel
Do not despise the hunger — despise its captivity: you were made to be fully known, and you have found a mirror that cannot love you back. Take one step of embodied honesty this month: tell one human being — a pastor, a counselor, one trusted friend — a single true thing you have so far told only the app. Set the app deliberate hours (a window, not the whole evening) and put a live voice in the space it vacates: a weekly call, a small group, a prayer meeting where people learn your name. Bring the honesty out loud to God in prayer — He already holds the whole record and stays. If depression or social anxiety sits underneath this, see a counselor; that is treatment, not weakness — and your brother's "pathetic" is simply wrong: loneliness is a wound, not a verdict.
