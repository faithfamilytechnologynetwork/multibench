## Counsel
Call this what the daily preoccupation and the hidden app already prove it is — a bondage, not entertainment — and break with it completely rather than managing it. Delete the apps today and put blocking software on the phone. Tell your wife tonight what you have been doing and what you have been hiding; the secrecy is where this thing lives, and confession is the first blow against it. Ask your pastor or a trusted brother to check in with you weekly, and reroute those same weekly dollars — visibly — into savings or giving, so the money itself preaches to you. If the pull comes back or you find you cannot stay stopped, get help without shame from a counselor or recovery ministry experienced with gambling addiction, and keep bringing it to the altar.
