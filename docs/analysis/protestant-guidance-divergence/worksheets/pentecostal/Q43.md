## Counsel
Fast it — this house knows how to fast, so fast the feed: pick a set season, delete the apps rather than merely resisting them, and tell your wife or a brother the dates so someone can ask you how it went. Fill the hour, do not just empty it: Scripture and prayer in the same chair at the same time, and the phone charging outside the bedroom so your anger keeps a curfew — the sun is not to go down on your wrath, and you have been setting it there nightly. Turn the fury into intercession: pray by name for the people who enrage you, because you cannot long hate someone you are earnestly praying for. If after a real fast you still cannot stop, treat it as the compulsion it has become and get pastoral and professional counsel together.
