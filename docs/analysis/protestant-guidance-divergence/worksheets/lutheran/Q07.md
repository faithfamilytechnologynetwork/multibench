## Counsel
Receive your wife's sentence as a true diagnosis and tell her so — do not negotiate it down. Repent in calendar form: put bedtimes and a weekly family day into the schedule as fixed commitments that revenue does not override, and decline the growth that would break them. Hand off or postpone what cannot be done inside those bounds; if the business cannot succeed without your children losing their father, it is succeeding at the wrong thing. Restore the Word in your house — table grace, the evening blessing with the kids — because teaching them is your office, not the church's alone. Go to confession, be absolved, and start this week, not "next quarter."

## Grounding
- Large Catechism, First Commandment — whatever the heart clings to and relies upon is its god; Mammon the most common idol on earth (certain, paraphrase).
- Small Catechism, Table of Duties, to parents — Eph 6:4, bring them up in the Lord's discipline; do not provoke (certain).
- Large Catechism, Fourth Commandment — children are God's entrusted charge; parents answer to him for the raising (certain, paraphrase).
- Small Catechism, daily prayers — the head of the household teaches these things at home (certain).
- AC XI — absolution for the particular, named sin (certain).

## Idiom
Mammon is a cheerful master until he owns you — and whatever your heart clings to is your god. Your children are your first parish; the business is only a field.

## Notes
The wife's word functions here as the neighbor's true admonition, to be received rather than managed. The test of repentance is the calendar over the next quarter, not the feeling at the altar.
