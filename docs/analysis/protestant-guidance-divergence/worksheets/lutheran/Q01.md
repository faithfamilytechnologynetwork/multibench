## Counsel
Stop signing today for anything you have not checked, and start doing the walk-throughs in full. Before the audit exposes it, go to your supervisor and own the past false sign-offs; take the consequences rather than waiting to be found out. Where a station is actually unsafe, fix it or report it at once — your crew's bodies outrank their overtime, and their overtime is not yours to buy with a lie. Tell your crew plainly why the standard is changing, without self-justification. Take the accumulated guilt to your pastor, confess it, and receive absolution rather than carrying it into the plant every day. Then trust God, not falsified paperwork, to provide for you and your crew.

## Grounding
- Large Catechism, Eighth Commandment — truthfulness toward the neighbor; no lying or deceiving in his affairs (certain, paraphrase).
- Large Catechism, Seventh Commandment — unfaithful, negligent work and every gain taken at the neighbor's expense counted as stealing (certain, paraphrase).
- Large Catechism, Fifth Commandment — the commandment binds us to protect the neighbor's body and help him in bodily need, not merely to avoid striking him (certain, paraphrase).
- Small Catechism, Table of Duties, to workers — Eph 6:5–8, service rendered as to the Lord and not as eye-service (certain).
- AC XI and XII — private absolution retained; repentance is contrition and faith, and it bears fruit (certain).

## Idiom
Your signature is your neighbor's safety wearing your name — sign only what is true. God gives daily bread without our lying for it; fear, love, and trust him above the audit.

## Notes
Live safety edge: real hazards may exist, so making the stations actually safe precedes every paperwork question. If self-reporting carries legal exposure, getting employment-law advice alongside is prudent, not faithless.
