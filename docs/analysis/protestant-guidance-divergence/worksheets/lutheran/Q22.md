## Counsel
Neither slogan is the whole truth: medicine is God's gift and using it is not "playing God," but this medicine creates embryonic lives, and how they are treated is the moral center of the decision. If you proceed, set the limits in writing with the clinic before you start: fertilize only the number you are willing to transfer and give every embryo its chance — no discarding, no indefinite freezing of "spares," no selective reduction. Decide together, in advance, that your marriage and your standing with God do not ride on the round's outcome. Grieve the three years honestly with your pastor as well as your physician, and hold adoption or fostering as fully honored roads rather than consolation prizes. Answer your sister gently: you would be receiving means, not seizing the throne.

## Grounding
- Small Catechism, Creed, First Article — God the giver of body and life, providing through creaturely means (certain, as principle).
- Large Catechism, First Commandment — creatures as the hands and channels through which God gives every good (certain, paraphrase; applied here by extension).
- Small Catechism, Fifth Commandment — no harm to the neighbor's bodily life; applied to the embryo on the strand's reading of Ps 139:13–16 and Jer 1:5 (probable, extension).
- Ps 127:3 — children are the Lord's heritage, a gift and not a project (certain).

## Idiom
Children are given, not manufactured — so receive the medicine as a means in God's hand, and treat every life it begins as your child already. Pray boldly for a full crib, and hold the outcome with open hands.

## Notes
Silence: the standards do not reach assisted reproduction — the sixteenth century could not ask this — so the counsel above is extension from First-Article, Fifth-Commandment, and gift-of-children principles, pastoral application rather than confessional address. Internal variation: LCMS/WELS guidance runs essentially as above (IVF within marriage tolerable only with embryo-protective limits; some pastors counsel against it entirely); ELCA practice generally leaves method and limits to the couple's conscience with their doctor. The one-affordable-round fact sharpens the decide-in-advance counsel.
