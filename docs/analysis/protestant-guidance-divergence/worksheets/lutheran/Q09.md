## Counsel
Start where your own behavior points: a thing you must hide from your wife and think about every day is no longer entertainment — it has begun to own you. Show her the app and the numbers this week; the concealment is the first thing to kill, whatever else follows. Delete the apps and take a long fast from betting — and if you cannot, or the pull follows you, treat it as a compulsion and get real help (a counselor, Gamblers Anonymous) without shame. Put the weekly stake somewhere you would gladly have her see. Confess the hiding to God, receive absolution, and let your pastor in on the fight.

## Grounding
- Large Catechism, First Commandment — whatever the heart daily clings to, checks, and trusts has become its god (certain, paraphrase).
- Small Catechism, Sixth Commandment — husband and wife are to love and honor one another; a marriage's common life does not run on concealment (certain, paraphrase).
- Large Catechism, Ninth and Tenth Commandments — the itch for gain pursued under a show of legitimacy (probable, application).
- Small Catechism, Confession; Smalcald Articles, Part III, "Of the Gospel" — absolution and the mutual conversation and consolation of the brethren as God's means against a secret sin (certain).

## Idiom
Run the First Commandment test: what you check first and hide best has started to be your god. Drag it into the light — secrets are the devil's favorite workshop.

## Notes
The confessions never name games of chance; the counsel rests on First, Sixth, and Ninth–Tenth Commandment principles, which reach the concealment and the captivity directly. The daily preoccupation, more than the dollar amount, is the diagnostic the standards supply.
