## Counsel
Do not laugh along and do not look away — silence in that room is a vote. Check on the junior teammate privately, tell him that what is happening to him is not right, and ask what support he wants. Speak to the manager alone first, respectfully and specifically, naming the behavior and its cost. If it continues, take dates and facts to the manager's superior or HR — the office that exists for exactly this. Commit the job fear to God before you act, and then act; if speaking costs you, that is a cross borne in a good work, and God has fed his people through leaner things than this town.

## Grounding
- Large Catechism, Eighth Commandment — not merely to avoid slander but to defend the neighbor, speak well of him, and put the best construction on things; private admonition before public (certain, paraphrase).
- Large Catechism, Fifth Commandment — guilt attaches to failing to help and defend the neighbor when we could (certain, paraphrase).
- Large Catechism, First Commandment — fear, love, and trust in God above every earthly security (certain).
- Lord's Prayer, Fourth Petition — daily bread is God's to give, with or without this employer (certain).
- Small Catechism, Table of Duties, to masters — Eph 6:9, authority warned against threatening (certain).

## Idiom
The Eighth Commandment does not just muzzle your tongue; it posts you in your neighbor's corner. Fear, love, and trust God above your paycheck, and stand up for the man.

## Notes
The ordered sequence (private word, then proper channels) is commanded prudence, not licensed delay; if a private word would endanger the junior colleague further, going straight to the proper office is the faithful route.
