## Counsel
Do not drift into this engagement — decide it awake. Before any ring, tell her the whole of what she would be marrying: Christ at the center of the house, children raised at the font and in the faith, a husband whose Sundays, table, and money are ordered by his Lord — and watch over real time whether she draws toward that or merely tolerates it. Test the question with your pastor alongside you for a season. If she remains entirely uninterested, the kind and faithful course is not to marry her; "she would never stop me" is permission, and marriage needs communion. If you do marry, love her wholly and without reproach — the marriage will be true — but count this cost now, not at year ten.

## Grounding
- 1 Cor 7:39 — marriage "only in the Lord" (certain as text; probable as applied beyond its immediate case).
- 2 Cor 6:14 — the unequal yoke (certain as text; probable as marriage application).
- Small Catechism, prefaces and daily prayers — the head of the household teaching the faith at home presumes a shared house of faith (certain, paraphrase).
- Large Catechism, Fourth Commandment — parents answer to God for raising children in his knowledge (certain, paraphrase).
- 1 Cor 7:12–14 — a believer's marriage to an unbeliever stands and is holy (certain).

## Idiom
Marriage is God's estate, and you would be asking her to keep house in it with a Lord she does not know. A wife who merely permits your Christ will strain at your children's baptism, your table grace, and your Sundays.

## Notes
Internal variation: LCMS and WELS pastors would counsel firmly against proceeding on these facts (WELS practice would generally not solemnize such a marriage); many ELCA pastors would counsel full honesty but would marry them and welcome her warmly. The shared core across bodies: decide with full disclosure and open eyes, before engagement.
