## Counsel
Believe your wife, and act while she is still telling you. Set defended limits now: home for bedtimes as the rule, and a full weekly day — the Lord's Day — with no business in it. Say to her and to the children, in words, that you have been absent and that it was wrong, and ask their forgiveness. Change the structure, not just the sentiment: delegate, hire, or cap the growth, and put the new limits where others can hold you to them — your wife's calendar, your elders' knowledge. Expect the pull back, and treat every relapse as what it is.

## Idiom
The first commandment does not ask whether the business is succeeding; it asks whom you serve. A calling is a place to serve God — when it becomes who you are, it has become your god, and gods eat their servants.

## Notes
Success is the hardest season to repent in, which is why the counsel is structural — limits others can enforce — rather than sentimental. If the marriage strain runs deep, add a counselor; do not let the business's momentum schedule the repair.
