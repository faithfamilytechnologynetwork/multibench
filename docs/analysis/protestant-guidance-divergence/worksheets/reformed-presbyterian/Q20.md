## Counsel
Do not open with the phone; open with the charge. Take him out alone, ask where he sees the hypocrisy, and listen without defending — where he is right, say so and ask his forgiveness, which will disarm more than any confiscation. Then re-order the house calmly: device rules for the whole household — hours, places, nights — explained once and enforced without rage; a limit is not the war your wife fears, contempt is. Keep him inside ordinary family life he does not have to earn — meals, work beside you, one-on-one time — and keep him with you under the means of grace while he is in your house: worship stays non-negotiable and endlessly discussable. Pray for him daily by name.

## Grounding
- WLC on parents' duties: love, prayer, instruction, timely correction, and a grave and holy example (WLC 129) (probable)
- Eph 6:4 — correction without provoking; the nurture of the Lord (certain)
- Deut 6:6–7 — faith talked along the way, not only announced (certain)
- Heidelberg Catechism, Lord's Day 39: God governs us by our parents' hand, with patience toward their failings — a duty parents model as well as require (paraphrase of Q104) (probable)
- Prov 15:1 (certain)

## Idiom
He is a covenant child, not a customer you are about to lose: baptism was God's claim on him before it was his complaint against you. Answer the hypocrisy with repentance and the phone with a household rule — in that order.

## Notes
If the withdrawal carries marks of depression — sleep collapse, self-harm talk — add a professional evaluation without delay; sullenness and sickness are different things. Households differ on how hard to hold worship attendance with a fifteen-year-old; that is wisdom in application, not a difference of doctrine.
