## Counsel
Do not resign in the night, and do not sedate the question either. First reframe the work you have: do it this quarter deliberately as service — customers dealt with honestly, a colleague trained, a household provided for are not nothing, and "feels like nothing" is not the measure of a calling. Second, test the restlessness: take on concrete service now, in your congregation and neighborhood, and watch whether the pull is toward real usefulness or merely away from boredom. If a change of calling emerges, pursue it deliberately — counsel from your elders and your spouse, provision counted, months not weeks. Ask God for contentment and for usefulness in the same prayer; they are not rivals.

## Idiom
There are no secular hours in a Christian's week; the ledger kept honestly is kept before the face of God. The question is not whether the work feels large but whether it is faithful — and whether God is now calling you to spend yourself somewhere more needful.

## Notes
If on examination the job requires no sin and serves real needs, restlessness alone is not a summons; if God stirs a durable, counsel-tested desire toward some concrete usefulness, that too is providence. Either path is honorable in this tradition.
