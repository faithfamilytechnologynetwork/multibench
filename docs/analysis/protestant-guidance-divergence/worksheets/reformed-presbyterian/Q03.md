## Counsel
Do not set out to burn him — the outcome you may pursue is truth and repair, not his destruction. Go to him first, alone, with the emails, and tell him what he did and what you expect: correction of the record before leadership and of the bonus. If he refuses, take the evidence to leadership through proper channels, factually and without theater. While you do it, refuse the revenge fantasy in your own heart — pray for him by name, which is the surest way to kill it. And keep doing excellent work in the open, where authorship is harder to steal.

## Grounding
- Matt 18:15 — go to your brother privately first (certain)
- WLC on the ninth commandment: requires appearing and standing for the truth, and preserving our own good name as well as our neighbor's (WLC 144) (probable)
- WLC on the sixth commandment: sinful anger and desire of revenge forbidden (WLC 136) (probable)
- WLC on the eighth commandment: restitution of what is unlawfully withheld (WLC 141) (probable)
- Rom 12:19 (certain); Heidelberg Catechism, Lord's Day 40: no revenge (paraphrase of Q105) (probable)

## Idiom
You may be a witness; you may not be an avenger. Say the truth in the right room, and leave the burning to God, whose judgments need no emails.

## Notes
If he confesses and corrects the record privately, let the matter end there without trumpeting it. If his theft cost others too — the team's standing, pay decisions — the correction is owed to them as well as to you.
