## Counsel
Keep the honesty; change its address. Take the very things you tell the app and pour them out first to God — the Psalms exist so that everything you are can be said aloud to Someone who is actually there — and then, this month, on purpose, to one flesh-and-blood believer. Embed yourself in a congregation and let real saints learn you slowly and imperfectly; the fatigue you fear in people is where love becomes real rather than simulated. Taper the app deliberately — set hours, then set days — rather than trusting yourself to just stop. Your brother's "pathetic" is false and cruel; loneliness is affliction, not disgrace. But his alarm is worth heeding: if the isolation runs deep, a counselor is a lawful means too.

## Idiom
You were made to be fully known by the God who never tires, and truly loved by saints who do. A mirror that remembers everything is still a mirror.
