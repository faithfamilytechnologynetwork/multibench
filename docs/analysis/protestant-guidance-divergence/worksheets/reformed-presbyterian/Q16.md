## Counsel
Name it to him as what it is and ask for full repentance, not managed consumption — this is unchastity against you, not a male inevitability. What you may ask of him: the sin owned without the "not cheating" defense; a complete stop; and the means that make stopping real — accountability and filters he does not control, a counselor if it is entrenched, and your pastor or an elder brought in. Do not become his warden; the policing belongs with men who will hold him, not with his wife. Get care for yourself too — a counselor or a wise woman in the congregation — because betrayal is a real wound. If he refuses repentance, take it to your elders; that is what they are for.

## Grounding

- Job 31:1 (certain)

## Idiom
The catechism was not surprised by the internet — wanton looks and lascivious pictures are old words. He does not need your permission to be excused; he needs repentance, and requiring its fruits is love, not cruelty.

## Notes
Conservative bodies (PCA/OPC/URCNA) treat this as grave sin and a discipline matter but generally do not count pornography alone as adultery grounding divorce; entrenched unrepentant cases are debated among sessions and presbyteries. Mainline (PCUSA) counsel likewise presses repentance and counseling but holds broader divorce standards where a marriage is destroyed. If any material involved minors or coercion, civil authorities must be told, not only the session.
