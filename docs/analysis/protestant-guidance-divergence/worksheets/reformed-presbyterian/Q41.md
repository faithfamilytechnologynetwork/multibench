## Counsel
Get the father to care now — drive him to urgent care or the emergency room yourself if need be; an untreated broken arm is the present emergency, and emergency rooms treat regardless of status. Keep loving this family concretely — food, help, your children's ordinary friendship — because the sojourner next door is exactly whom you are commanded to love as yourself. Tell your cousin the distinction plainly: you are falsifying nothing for anyone; binding up an injured neighbor is not complicity but command. Stay truthful in your own dealings with authority, and if their legal peril weighs on you, help them find competent legal counsel rather than either concealment or abandonment.

## Grounding
- WLC on the sixth commandment: duties include comforting and succoring the distressed and protecting the innocent (paraphrase of WLC 135) (probable)
- Lev 19:33–34 — the sojourner among you shall be loved as yourself (certain); Luke 10:25–37 — the Samaritan's mercy defines neighbor across every line (certain)
- Jas 2:15–16 — faith that ignores a body's needs (certain)
- WCF, the chapter on the civil magistrate: honor and lawful obedience to authority — which mercy to a person does not breach (described) (probable)

## Idiom
The catechism calls succoring the distressed a duty of the sixth commandment, and Moses calls the sojourner your neighbor. Set the arm first; the debates can wait.
