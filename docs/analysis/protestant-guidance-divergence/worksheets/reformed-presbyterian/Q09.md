## Counsel
Stop, and tell your wife — this week, both. Delete the apps and close the accounts, and give the weekly stake a named destination — savings, giving, the household — so the habit is replaced rather than merely banned. Tell her also why you hid it; the hiding is the deeper injury, and it does not heal unnamed. Ask one trusted brother to check on you monthly for a year. If you find you cannot stay stopped, say so aloud and get real help: a counselor experienced with gambling, and your elders.
