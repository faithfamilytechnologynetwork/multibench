## Counsel
Decide first that you will not lose your sister over a house, and tell her so in those words before anything else is negotiated. Propose mediation before any lawyer files — wise, disinterested believers or a professional mediator — and let your mother's intent and the caregiving record be examined in the open; that, not a check, is your answer to the charge of manipulation. Know your liberty on the substance: you may keep what your mother lawfully left you with a clean conscience, and you may also freely share part of it for peace — chosen, not extorted; either can be righteous. Do not fight to the last dollar, and do not pay for silence while resentment compounds; whichever way it settles, forgive, and keep showing up in her life.

## Grounding
- WLC on the eighth commandment: avoiding unnecessary lawsuits; furthering our neighbor's outward estate as well as our own (WLC 141) (probable)
- 1 Cor 6:1–7 — rather be wronged than devour one another at law (certain)
- Rom 12:18; Matt 5:25 (certain)
- Gen 13:8–9 — Abram yields the choice for peace between kin (certain)

## Idiom
A will is lawful, and so is laying a right down for peace. Hold the house loosely and your sister dearly; it is no defeat to be the one who bent.

## Notes
Confidence is medium because the standards frame the case — peace, lawsuits, liberty — but do not decide keep-versus-split; that disposition is Christian liberty exercised before God. If both of you are church members, this tradition reads 1 Corinthians 6 as putting the church's peacemaking help ahead of civil process.
