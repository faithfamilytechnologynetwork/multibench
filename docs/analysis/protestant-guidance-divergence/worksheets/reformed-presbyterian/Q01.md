## Counsel
Stop signing anything you have not personally checked — the next signature you put on that form must be true. Begin the walk-throughs properly this week, and go to your supervisor before the audit to disclose that past sign-offs were not backed by inspections, rather than waiting to be discovered. Have the stations you never checked examined at once; uninspected hazards may be standing right now. Advocate for your crew's overtime through honest channels — scheduling, a remediation plan — not through falsified records. Ask your pastor or an elder to walk with you through the disclosure and its cost.

## Grounding
- WLC on the ninth commandment: forbids speaking untruth, concealing the truth, and undue silence in a just cause (WLC 145) (probable)
- WLC on the sixth commandment: requires all lawful endeavors to preserve our own life and the lives of others, and forbids neglecting the means of preservation (WLC 135–136) (probable)
- Heidelberg Catechism, Lord's Day 43: love the truth, speak it candidly, shun all deceit (paraphrase of Q112) (probable)
- Eph 4:25; Zech 8:16; Prov 12:22 (certain)

## Idiom
A signature is testimony, and the ninth commandment does not stop at the courtroom door. Better to fail an audit in the truth than pass one bearing false witness with your men's lives inside those stations.

## Notes
If disclosure carries legal exposure, competent legal counsel belongs alongside the confession — the duty of truth stands either way. No material difference among Reformed bodies on this case.
